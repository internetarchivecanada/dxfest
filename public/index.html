Twelve years of investigative film

The Double Exposure Investigative Film Festival has brought filmmakers and investigative journalists together in Washington, DC every year since 2015. This explorer gathers its 226 films, 267 forum sessions and 1,319 people from the festival’s twelve editions, so you can follow a filmmaker, a reporter or a subject across the years.
