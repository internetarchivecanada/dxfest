Age of Consent

Friday, October 11, 2019 · 11:00 AM–12:15 PM · Beverly Snow Room, Eaton Hotel

Inherent in the role of journalist or filmmaker is the desire to tell a story, often toward a greater good. Whether with notebook or camera lens, the storyteller is framing the investigation, sometimes spending long years immersed in a place or situation, sometimes parachuting in. Increasingly, however, subjects are questioning the authority of outsiders to tell their stories. They are demanding a say over how they are represented and objecting when they are not consulted. Foundations are taking notice, and developing policies to promote community involvement in the creative or reporting process. This shift marks a departure from, and challenge to, the traditional journalistic stance of the neutral investigator.

With Jacqueline Olive, Juliana Schatz, Courtney Symone Staton and Shayna Wilson. Moderated by Natalie Bullock Brown.
