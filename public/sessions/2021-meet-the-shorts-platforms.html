Panel · Double Exposure 2021

Meet the Shorts Platforms

Wednesday, October 13, 2021 · 11:30am–1:00pm

Funding & distribution

So you’ve completed your short documentary film – what’s next? Short documentary is in a digital renaissance, with opportunities to reach audiences constantly evolving, but the trip from production to platform remains unsteady or unclear for many journalists and filmmakers. Join commissioning editors and producers of a wide range of international news outlets, broadcasters and digital distribution platforms in a conversation about the ins and outs of reaching an audience through their programming. This panel discussion will shed light on next steps, best practices, and how to partner in distribution of your short documentary film.

This panel is presented in partnership with IF/Then Shorts.
