NFTs & CRYPTOCURRENCIES

Friday, October 15, 2021 · 4:30pm–6:00pm

Money, banks & business

In recent months, we’ve watched in amazement as art works, music, even a New York Times column, fetched hundreds of thousands of dollars as NFTs. One news organization has launched its work on the metaverse, and is offering NFTs as a way to finance its reporting. Do NFTs offer a viable way to fund journalism or filmmaking? We’ll hear from the experts on NFTs and cryptocurrencies, who will break it down and tell you whether NFTs represent a passing fad or a viable way for content creators like journalists and filmmakers to profit directly from their work.
