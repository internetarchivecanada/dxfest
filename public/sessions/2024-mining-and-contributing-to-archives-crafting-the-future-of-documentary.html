Mining and Contributing to Archives: Crafting the Future of Documentary Storytelling

Friday, November 8, 2024 · 1:00 PM–2:15 PM · The Johns Hopkins University Bloomberg Center

Archives & historyRace & civil rights

Archives offer filmmakers tangible connections to the events, cultures, and individuals they aim to illuminate, encompassing historical footage, photographs, personal letters, and official documents. By skillfully integrating these archival materials, filmmakers breathe life into stories, providing audiences with a vivid and poignant glimpse into our collective history. Hear from the directors of three films, Acts of Reparation, Bombshell and American Coup: Wilmington 1898, as they explore how to effectively work with archives to enhance storytelling.
