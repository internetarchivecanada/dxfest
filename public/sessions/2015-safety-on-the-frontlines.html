Panel · Double Exposure 2015

Safety on the Frontlines

Thursday, October 1, 2015 · 2:30 PM–4:00 PM · Woolly Mammoth Theatre, Main Theatre

Race & civil rightsLegal & safety for reporters

Pursuit of a story can put reporters and filmmakers at physical risk in the field. Navigating the balance between storytelling and personal safety can make the difference between getting the killer shot and living to tell the tale. This panel will explore essential training and preparation for entering an area of conflict, and the measures that reporters and filmmakers can take to protect themselves.
