Story Based Inquiry

Wednesday, October 14, 2020 · 11:00am–12:15pm

This workshop, presented in two parts, condenses the famed Story-Based Inquiry method, which has been successfully used by thousands of journalists, academics and NGO researchers since its publication by UNESCO in 2009. It gives you the basics for starting, deepening, organizing and writing an investigative report or story. The method can be used for feature writing or documentary filmmaking as well as reportage. Its language and approach have been integrated into leading journalism organisations and NGOs worldwide.
