Lunchtime Conversation: One Story Two Forms

Thursday, October 13, 2022 · 1:00 PM–1:45 PM · District Architecture Center

Meg Shutzer and Rachel Lauren Mueller, filmmakers behind 8 DAYS AT WARE which is having its world premiere at DX22, reflect on the opportunities, challenges and complexities of creating two simultaneous works of investigative journalism on the same subject— a short film and a long form print piece for a major national publication. When do you risk taking out the camera? How do you represent, or not, the scope of an investigation in a visual medium? When there is moral urgency to publish, but the timelines of print and film don’t align, how do you act ethically?
