The Art of Exposure with Halcyon House Fellows

Saturday, October 12, 2019 · 1:00 PM–3:00 PM · Eaton Cinema

Each year, Double Exposure celebrates the vibrant intersection of investigative journalism and film. The cinematic lens, though, is but one medium in which investigative journalism influences and shapes the cultural landscape. This year’s symposium incorporates podcasts that have broken new investigative ground on their own. With this session, we’re proud to partner with Halcyon House Arts Lab. This session will present Halcyon fellows-in-residence whose work is fueled by deep reporting on timely issues. Through performance, photography, poetry, and other forms of art they tackle the quest to expose and investigate from new directions. The artists will present segments from their work in a series of presentations, and be on hand to take questions.
