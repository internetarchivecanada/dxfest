Generative AI: Toward Best Practices in Documentary Production

Thursday, November 7, 2024 · 11:30 AM–12:45 PM · The Johns Hopkins University Bloomberg Center

Technology & AIDocumentary craft

As the film industry continues to explore AI’s potential for speeding production and opening new creative realms, complex ethical challenges and implications are emerging apace. In this discussion, industry experts explore topics such as intellectual property rights, bias in AI algorithms, spotting fakes, media credibility, and the potential impact of AI on creative jobs in filmmaking. Join the conversation and foster a deeper understanding of how filmmakers can responsibly integrate AI into their projects while upholding ethical, artistic, and–where applicable–journalistic integrity.
