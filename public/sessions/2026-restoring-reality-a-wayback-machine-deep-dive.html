Restoring Reality: A Wayback Machine Deep Dive

Saturday, November 7, 2026 · 11:30am–12:45pm · Martin Luther King Jr. Memorial Library

Archives & history

The Wayback Machine holds hundreds of billions of archived web pages — but knowing it exists and knowing how to actually find what you’re looking for are two different skills. This hands-on workshop, led by the Wayback Machine’s own director, walks participants through the practical mechanics of searching the archive: how to locate a deleted government page, track how a public statement changed over time, and verify that a record once existed even after it’s gone from the live web.

What you’ll leave with: the practical ability to find internet records yourself.
