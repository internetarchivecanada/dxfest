The Morning After: Analyzing the 2024 Presidential Election Results

Thursday, November 7, 2024 · 9:30 AM–10:15 AM · The Johns Hopkins University Bloomberg Center

Democracy & elections

As the United States emerges from a staggeringly fraught presidential election, marked by polarization and concerns for the future of democracy, we open this year's festival by hearing from those who have been most closely watching the battle for votes. What does this election, in conduct and results, tell us about where Americans are as a people, how values are shifting, and where the nation is headed? Can we even speak of "a people" in this moment of division? For the journalists and filmmakers listening in, how do we tell the stories of these fractious times?
