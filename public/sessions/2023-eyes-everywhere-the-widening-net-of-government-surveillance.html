Panel · Double Exposure 2023

Eyes Everywhere: The Widening Net of Government Surveillance

Friday, November 3, 2023 · 2:30 PM–3:45 PM · Martin Luther King, Jr. Memorial Library — Auditorium

Surveillance & privacyMigration & borders

In this panel, we explore the expanding threats to the privacy of our devices and hard drives through mass extraction techniques, especially by law enforcement and border authorities. The practice has far-reaching implications for fundamental rights, including privacy and the presumption of innocence, but it also poses a particular peril for journalists and filmmakers seeking to protect their sources and material, along with ethical questions about the use of information gleaned through these tactics.
