Pro Bono Legal Clinic

Thursday, October 14, 2021 · 11:15am–1:00pm

Whistleblowers & leaksPublic records & FOIAPress freedom & journalists at riskLegal & safety for reporters

The Pro Bono Legal Clinic offers investigative storytellers lacking legal representation the opportunity to connect with experts who can knowledgeably discuss legal challenges they are confronting. Leading attorneys in the areas of First Amendment law, privacy and libel, Freedom of Information, whistleblower protection, copyright and intellectual property will be on hand. Attorneys will brief participants on case law and trends relevant to the problems that journalists and filmmakers have articulated upon registering for the clinic, and field questions from them. Attorneys have also agreed to consider representing select participants in need of counsel on an ongoing basis pro bono.

Passholders who pre-register for the clinic will receive a link, where they can describe any specific legal challenges they may be facing, to help guide the discussion. Seating is limited and available on a first-come first-served basis, with priority to those who pre-register.
