Staying Safe: Identifying, Understanding, and Preparing for Risks

Friday, October 15, 2021 · 11:30am–1:00pm

War, conflict & the militaryLegal & safety for reportersMiddle East & South Asia

The James W. Foley Legacy Foundation (JWFLF) was founded in 2014 after the kidnap and murder of James Foley, an American freelance journalist, while reporting in Syria. JWFLF advocates for the freedom of all Americans held hostage abroad and promotes the safety of journalists worldwide. In terms of safety, both physical and psychological, the threats confronting journalists continue to grow at home and abroad. This discussion will focus on the importance of developing safe journalistic habits, understanding journalists’ rights, specific risks faced by photojournalists, journalists of color, and female journalists, and strategies and techniques to survive wrongful detention and/or hostage-taking abroad.

This panel is presented in partnership with the James W. Foley Legacy Foundation.
