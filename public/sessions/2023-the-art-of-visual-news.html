The Art of Visual News

Thursday, November 2, 2023 · 2:15 PM–3:30 PM · Martin Luther King, Jr. Memorial Library — Event Space, 5th Floor

Documentary craftThe news business

In an era shaped by the omnipresence of social media and a burgeoning demand for greater inclusivity in journalism, a fresh wave of younger audiences is gravitating towards unconventional modes of visual news consumption. This panel seeks to delve into the realm of innovative storytelling methods that are blossoming within newsrooms eager to forge deeper connections with a media-savvy demographic. As this ongoing experimentation gives rise to a variety of diverse narrative forms and styles, it prompts introspection within news organizations on essential matters like community engagement. Furthermore, a renewed commitment to transparency in the reporting process, fostering a more profound partnership with the public, is setting the course for this exciting transformation in the world of news. Moderated by Mishi Ebrahim.
