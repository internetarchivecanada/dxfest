Distribution in Flux: Reimagining and Thriving in the New Era

Friday, November 8, 2024 · 2:30 PM–3:45 PM · The Johns Hopkins University Bloomberg Center

Funding & distribution

Today’s rapidly evolving distribution landscape is reshaping the film industry. This panel of experts analyzes the impact of digital vs. theatrical platforms, changes in audience behavior, and the contraction of what networks and streamers are acquiring, particularly in terms of genres. Listen to these panelists as they explore innovative distribution models that are emerging, and the role of film festivals in enhancing visibility. Gain valuable insights into navigating the complexities of film distribution, and feel empowered to make informed decisions for your own projects amidst these shifting dynamics.
