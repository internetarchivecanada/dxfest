Ask Me Anything: Meet the Funders

Friday, October 11, 2019 · 2:45 PM–4:00 PM · Beverly Snow Room, Eaton Hotel

Everything you always wanted to know from funders but were afraid to ask. We’ll be collecting anonymous questions from festival attendees and posing them to the assembled funders, who have promised to answer whatever’s on your mind, no holds barred. Bring it on . . .
