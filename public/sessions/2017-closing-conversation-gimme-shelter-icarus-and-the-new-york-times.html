Closing Conversation - Gimme Shelter: Icarus and The New York Times

Thursday, October 19, 2017 · 4:15 PM–5:30 PM · National Union Building

Whistleblowers & leaksRussia & Eastern Europe

A series of New York Times interviews introduced the protagonist of ICARUS—the whistleblower at the center of the film who exposed widespread doping of Russian athletes—even before the film’s celebrated debut at the 2017 Sundance Film Festival.

While documentarians typically find inspiration in newspaper accounts, ICARUS turned that convention on its head. Filmmaker Bryan Fogel and producer Dan Cogan sought in advance journalistic coverage to protect their film subject, Grigory Rodchenkov. They turned to The New York Times reporter Rebecca Ruiz, who had been in touch with Rodchenkov before he came to the United States, and offered exclusive access to a prized source under their protection.
