Workshop · Double Exposure 2019

Finding Character

Saturday, October 12, 2019 · 2:00 PM–4:00 PM · Crystal Room, Eaton Hotel

Documentary craft

From the unstoppable growth of podcasting to the latest binge-worthy formats in storytelling to new ways of engaging your audience, all of them require a creative approach to your investigation. Join Susanne Reber for a wide-ranging conversation to explore how you can prepare yourself to think in characters, scenes, and sequences and allow your story to make it into video or audio and sometimes both. We will talk about the mindset, casting your characters early on, and finding creative ways to solve familiar challenges in investigative reporting: complexity, data sets, and numbers. How can you frame your story to engage your audience, hold its attention and build their involvement, by using available technology to start a two-way conversation?
