Panel · Double Exposure 2015

Truth to Power: Trapdoors

Thursday, October 1, 2015 · 1:00 PM–2:30 PM · Woolly Mammoth Theatre, Main Theatre

Journalists and filmmakers are increasingly taking on powerful corporate interests and institutions. What happens when the targets of investigations go after the investigator? What are the areas of vulnerability, and how can reporters and visual storytellers protect themselves?
