Workshop · Double Exposure 2019

Solutions Journalism for Investigative Film

Saturday, October 12, 2019 · 11:45 AM–1:15 PM · Crystal Room, Eaton Hotel

Funding & distribution

Participants in this interactive workshop will learn how to use solutions journalism to add audience appeal and real-world impact to an investigative film. They’ll learn: The What: What is solutions journalism — and what is it not? The Why: How can solutions journalism make a documentary film stronger? The How: How do I find a solutions angle? How do I report on a solution without advocacy, cheerleading, do-gooding or PR? How do I integrate it into a problem-focused story?
