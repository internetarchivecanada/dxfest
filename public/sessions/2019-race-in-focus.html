Race In Focus

Thursday, October 10, 2019 · 9:30am–10:45am

Race & civil rightsAfrica

The fifth edition of DX kicked off with a timely conversation featuring Wesley Lowery, a Pulitzer-Prize winning national reporter for the Washington Post covering law enforcement, justice, race and politics, and, multi-award winning documentary filmmakers Marco Williams and Michèle Stephenson. Together, they examined the representation of race and the black experience through film and journalism. New scholarship arising from the 400th anniversary of 1619 — when the first pirate ship delivered more than 20 African captives to the Virginia shore — suggests that the legacy of bondage and racial subjugation shapes nearly every aspect of modern life, from labor relations to law, from education to the economy, from art to ethics, and much more. Investigative storytellers in print and film are challenging the white gaze and its underlying presumptions, bringing new questions and lines of investigation to the stories of our times.
