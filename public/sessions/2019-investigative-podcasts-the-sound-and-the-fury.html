Investigative Podcasts: The Sound and the Fury

Thursday, October 10, 2019 · 11:00 AM–12:15 PM · Beverly Snow Room, Eaton Hotel

Health & medicineThe news business

It’s been five years since the investigative podcast that started it all, Serial, emerged on the scene, and since then the format has exploded with some of the most captivating investigative storytelling to be found in any medium. Hear from creators and producers of some of the very best in audio investigations today, from West Cork, named after the quaint Irish region that became the unlikely backdrop to the 1996 murder of Sophie Toscan du Plantier, a French film producer, to the multi-season In the Dark which examines in electrifying and infuriating detail a child-abduction in season one and a wrongful imprisonment in season two, and reveals the inner machinations of the investigative process as much as a judicial system gone awry. And finally, Dr. Death, which examines charismatic neurosurgeon Christopher Duntsch who was convicted of gross malpractice after thirty-one of his patients were left seriously injured after he operated on them, and two patients died during his operation.
