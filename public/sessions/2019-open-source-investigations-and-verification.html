Workshop · Double Exposure 2019

Open Source Investigations and Verification

Saturday, October 12, 2019 · 10:00 AM–11:30 AM · Crystal Room, Eaton Hotel

Disinformation & truth

Publically available tools now make it possible for citizens and independent journalists to investigate and verify content. In this hands-on workshop, you will learn how to channel your inner Sherlock Holmes and use open source tools to verify content.
