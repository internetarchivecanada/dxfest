The New Transparency

Friday, October 11, 2019 · 1:15 PM–2:30 PM · Beverly Snow Room, Eaton Hotel

Disinformation & truth

For most of the last hundred years, trust resided in a name: of a reporter, of a news outlet, of a filmmaker or a studio. As New York University professor Jay Rosen has noted, that’s changing fast. In an age of widespread public mistrust of media and organized disinformation, the ability of readers and audiences to understand, and verify, the news gathering process has become key to credibility, replacing reputation the barometer of trust. That means

disclosing the assumptions and biases of journalists and filmmakers, unpacking the steps of an investigation and choices along the way and, even, welcoming non-journalists into the reporting process. Activists are showing readers and viewers how to cross-check information, how to mine social media and open source data, and how to trace the provenance of questionable images and information. Their goal: to produce more reliable and trustworthy stories, and more savvy consumers of media.

With Karim Amer, Jay Rosen, and Claire Wardle. Moderated by Harlo Holmes.
