Witness Or Activist? Democracy Under Siege

Friday, October 15, 2021 · 4:45pm–6:00pm

Democracy & elections

What is the role of journalists and filmmakers in a time of existential peril to democracy? Recent Supreme Court decisions on voting rights, state regulations aimed at creating obstacles for Black and brown people to vote, and dozens of court cases across the country ensure the question will only loom larger in the months and years ahead. With voting rights under fire across the country, is it the role of journalists and filmmakers to alone bear witness to the erosion of democracy’s pillars, or are they called upon to do more?
