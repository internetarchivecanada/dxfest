New Digital Security Platform for Filmmakers: a Walk-through and Training Session

Thursday, October 14, 2021 · 1:30pm–3:00pm

Legal & safety for reporters

Digital security experts Harlo Holmes and Olivia Martin will discuss the development of Digital Security for Filmmakers, a new resource for filmmakers developed by their team at Freedom of the Press Foundation and presented by production house Field of Vision. Film professionals face unique risks in protecting their data throughout production, whether it’s keeping the project under wraps while communicating with sensitive subjects, moving footage from the field to home base, or just keeping their work devices malware-free and backed-up. Holmes and Martin will walk through the Digital Security for Filmmakers platform, spotlighting a slice of the dozens of resources on footage protection, communication security, fieldwork, device protection, and more. During the walk-through, Homes and Martin will take opportunities to train on digital security best practices and risk assessment, so come with your questions and notebooks in-hand!
