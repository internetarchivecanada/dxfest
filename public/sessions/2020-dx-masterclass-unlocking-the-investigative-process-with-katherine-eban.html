DX Masterclass: Unlocking the Investigative Process with Katherine Eban

Wednesday, October 14, 2020 · 3:00pm–4:00pm

Terrorism & national securityMigration & bordersCareers & training

Katherine Eban is an investigative journalist, Vanity Fair contributing editor and Andrew Carnegie fellow. Her reporting on generic drug fraud, gun trafficking, and coercive interrogations by the CIA, has won international attention and numerous awards, and became the basis for the Amazon film, The Report. Eban will discuss her painstaking reporting process: what draws her to a subject; her steps for winning over sources and getting information; reporting against her starting hypothesis, and determining how best to tell the story.
