Panel · Double Exposure 2016

First Person Singular: Investigations Born through Personal Experience

Friday, October 7, 2016 · 10:30 AM–12:00 PM · National Press Club, Zenger Room

A brother’s murder in Scotland leads to combing through rubble in Libya, in a decades-long search for his killer. An assault on a child triggers an odyssey through the criminal justice system. A career spent behind the camera shooting footage from all around the world prompts the cameraperson to turn the lens on herself. Where deeply felt experience propels a search for answers, there is more than just another story at stake.

The quest poses an inherent challenge — to be both observer and observed, to live in the event and simultaneously step back. The results can prove startlingly personal and broadly illuminating at once, as singular as a fingerprint.
