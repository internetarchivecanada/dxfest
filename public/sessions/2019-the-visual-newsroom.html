Panel · DX² · Double Exposure 2019

The Visual Newsroom

Thursday, October 10, 2019 · 2:45 PM–4:00 PM · Beverly Snow Room, Eaton Hotel

The news business

Newsrooms are increasingly turning toward film as an essential medium for opening new dimensions in storytelling, unpacking investigations, and reaching different audiences. We’ll hear from newsrooms at the forefront of this shift.

With Katie Campbell, Lindsay Crouse, Lauren Feeney, Cassandra Giraldo, Amanda Pike and Zach Toombs. Moderated by Patricia Aufderheide.
