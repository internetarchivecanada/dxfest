Workshop: DIY Impact & Distribution for Short Docs

Saturday, November 1, 2025 · 10:00 AM–11:30 AM · Johns Hopkins University Bloomberg Center, 10th Floor

Technology & AIFunding & distribution

Today, traditional gatekeepers are fading and social platforms can offer investigative filmmakers the power to control their own distribution—and reach millions. In this session, we'll speak about how to integrate social video production into your larger vision. I'll show how Mother Jones has built loyal audiences on YouTube, TikTok, and Instagram by turning complex reporting into compelling short-form video. And how to use that to build an audience ready for your full-length feature.
