Defy Apathy! A Workshop to Build Public Trust in Journalism

Saturday, October 12, 2019 · 2:00 PM–4:00 PM · Crystal Room, Eaton Hotel

A hands-on session in which attendees of the symposium use creative tools to find solutions to truth decay and restoring public trust in the news.

The workshop is facilitated by Eric Matthies, filmmaker, and narrative design producer. Eric has a longstanding involvement in citizen advocacy for the press and experience convening disparate stakeholders in active sessions to build stable solutions for complex problems.

The session kicks off with a brief introduction/presentation of researched facts defining the problem and its core drivers. Then, each of our invited media representatives will engage in moderated discussion on the topic from their perspective, after which we’ll divide into groups and use narrative design tools and some punk ‘Do It Yourself’ energy to prototype art-based solutions.

The most important expert in the room is you – this is an opportunity where we working professionals and the public come together around our respective areas of expertise and concern to help build solutions. Rebuilding trust in journalism is not for journalism, it is for all of us – we are explicitly and intrinsically entangled with a need for trustworthy information.

For the majority of time in the session, we will split into working groups moderated by each of our experts and guided by the workshop facilitator. Taking advantage of the spectrum of creative tools available to us at Double Exposure will ensure a stimulating experience with a tangible outcome. What we make and how we define, design and execute it will be largely up to the convened group of participants. At the end of the session, everyone will have contributed to making contextual, illuminating and actionable content that each participant can share via their own platforms.
