Tending the Unspoken Wounds: The Role of Self-Reflection in Cultivating a Healing-Centered Ethic in Investigative Journalism & Documentary Filmmaking

Saturday, October 15, 2022 · 10:30 AM–12:00 PM · District Architecture Center

Documentary craft

Many in the industry find themselves standing (or struggling) at an exquisitely perplexing crossroad: how do we document and share the stories unfolding in a world which is experiencing such upheaval and uncertainty? How do we both bear witness and process the painful AND joyous events in the lives of our storytellers? How does one acknowledge and cope with vicarious trauma in a field which prioritizes (the myth of) objectivity as the gold standard of professionalism? This workshop will explore these questions from the lens of self-awareness, critical consciousness, and healing justice. This workshop will also highlight the work of the Documentary Accountability Working Group (DAWG)’s core values and framework for values, ethics and accountability in non-fiction filmmaking, with a particular focus on reflection as a first and essential phase of this important work.
