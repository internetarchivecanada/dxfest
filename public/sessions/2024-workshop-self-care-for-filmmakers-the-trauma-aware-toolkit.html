Workshop: Self-Care for Filmmakers: The Trauma Aware Toolkit

Saturday, November 9, 2024 · 10:00 AM–11:30 AM · The Johns Hopkins University Bloomberg Center

Women, gender & sexualityDocumentary craft

Journalists and filmmakers worldwide cover the most vulnerable people, while themselves contending with the impact of stress and trauma. The Dart Center for Journalism and Trauma has just released a new toolkit to help documentary filmmakers, journalists, and editors report on violence while taking care of themselves and colleagues. Judith Matloff, Dart’s senior advisor for safety, will introduce this new resource. She will field questions about planning for difficult stories, ethics, leadership, traumatic imagery, interview techniques, and reporting on vulnerable communities, children, and survivors of gender/identity attacks.
