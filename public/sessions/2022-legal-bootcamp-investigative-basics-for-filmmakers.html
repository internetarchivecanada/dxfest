Legal Bootcamp: Investigative Basics for Filmmakers

Saturday, October 15, 2022 · 11:30 AM–12:45 PM · District Architecture Center

Legal & safety for reportersCareers & training

A primer on legal rights and responsibilities for investigative journalists in print and film, and for filmmakers venturing into investigative territory. This session will familiarize you with recording laws and common legal issues documentarians can face while filming (e.g., intrusion, trespass), and provide tips on how to avoid them. Led by attorneys from the Reporters Committee for Freedom of the Press, this workshop will also discuss the shift toward more collaborative approaches with film protagonists, and the legal implications for investigative filmmakers. Workshop leaders will share legal resources that are available and field your questions at the end of the presentation.
