Workshop · Double Exposure 2025

Workshop Day

Saturday, November 1, 2025 · 9:30am–2:00pm · Johns Hopkins University Bloomberg Center

Careers & training

Workshop Day at Double Exposure is all about rolling up your sleeves and diving into immersive, skill-building experiences alongside fellow filmmakers and journalists. Whether you’re looking to master new techniques, sharpen your storytelling tools, or tackle the latest industry challenges,  these workshops give you actionable insights and hands-on learning. Please note that space for each workshop is limited and available on a first-come, first-served basis—so secure your spot early to make the most of these unique learning opportunities.
