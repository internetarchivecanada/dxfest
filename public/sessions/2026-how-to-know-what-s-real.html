Workshop · Double Exposure 2026

How to Know What’s Real

Saturday, November 7, 2026 · 11:30am–12:45pm · The Johns Hopkins University Bloomberg Center

This workshop teaches practical, everyday habits for checking whether what you’re looking at is what it claims to be: how to trace where an image or video actually came from, what warning signs suggest manipulation, and what questions to ask before you share something or believe it. Bring your phone. Bring your skepticism. Leave with tools you’ll actually use.
