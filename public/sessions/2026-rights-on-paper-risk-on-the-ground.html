Rights on Paper, Risk on the Ground

Saturday, November 7, 2026 · 10:00am–11:20am · The Johns Hopkins University Bloomberg Center

Legal & safety for reporters

Citizens who take on the work of journalism rarely have an institution or a legal team standing behind them when things go sideways. This workshop is built for that gap — covering both what the law actually protects and what it takes to stay safe in the moment when protection isn’t guaranteed. Participants work through real scenarios: securing devices and communications, recognizing the lines of the reporter’s role, documenting encounters with law enforcement, and reading a situation in real time.

What you’ll leave with: a working understanding of your rights on paper, how to judge what’s safe on the ground, and how to mitigate risk when you’re on your own.
