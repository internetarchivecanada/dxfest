Shorts: Breaking the Mold: Defying Conventional Narrative Shorts

Sunday, November 10, 2024 · 1:30 PM · Martin Luther King Jr. Memorial Library

This bold collection of documentary shorts takes a stand against prevailing narratives, challenging viewers to rethink accepted beliefs and confront uncomfortable truths. Each film pushes back against dominant ways of thinking—whether about politics, society, culture, or identity—inviting us to question assumptions and explore alternative perspectives. These stories prove that pushing boundaries is essential to understanding the world in all its complexity.
