Panel · Double Exposure 2019

#MeToo 2.0

Friday, October 11, 2019 · 9:30 AM–10:45 AM · Beverly Snow Room, Eaton Hotel

Women, gender & sexuality

The calling out of sexual predators, that began with women in the film industry unmasking Harvey Weinstein, has deepened and grown. Hear from journalists at the Miami Herald, whose dogged reporting forced prosecutors to reopen the Jeffrey Epstein case, and from the filmmakers behind “Surviving Jeffrey Epstein,” a new multi-part documentary from Lifetime (which borrows its nomenclature from Lifetime’s breakthrough multi-part documentary Surviving R. Kelly). This panel will examine the heroic efforts and scrupulous methods undertaken by investigative journalists and filmmakers to render visible, through print and film, predators “hiding in plain sight.”
