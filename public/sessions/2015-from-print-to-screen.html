Panel · Double Exposure 2015

From Print to Screen

Thursday, October 1, 2015 · 4:15 PM–5:45 PM · Woolly Mammoth Theatre, Main Theatre

Investigative stories that originated with the written word and were then reinterpreted for the screen. How much does the film rely on the original writing and how does the addition of the audio/visual component impact the story? Hear first-hand how such collaborations have worked and what was gained or lost in the transition for both the filmmakers and the journalists.
