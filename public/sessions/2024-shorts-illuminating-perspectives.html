Screening event · Double Exposure 2024

Shorts: Illuminating Perspectives

Friday, November 8, 2024 · 12:00 PM · Martin Luther King Jr. Memorial Library

This powerful collection of short documentaries offers an eye-opening exploration of the human condition. From intimate personal stories to broader societal issues, each film delves into unique perspectives on resilience, identity, and the shared experiences that connect us all. Prepare to be moved, challenged, and inspired as these filmmakers bring untold stories to light and push the boundaries of empathy and understanding.
