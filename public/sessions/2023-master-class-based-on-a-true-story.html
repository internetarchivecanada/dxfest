Master Class: Based on a True Story

Saturday, November 4, 2023 · 2:00 PM–3:00 PM · Martin Luther King, Jr. Memorial Library — Conference Room F, 4th Floor

Indigenous peoples & land

A master class from the writer’s room of “Alaska Daily,” the TV series about a state’s failure to prosecute the murders of indigenous women, set at a struggling daily newspaper. Join Pulitzer Prize-winning investigative journalist Mike Rezendes and Steinberg Playwright Award-winner Miranda Rose Hall for a workshop on adapting real-life investigative stories into narrative work for stage and screen.
