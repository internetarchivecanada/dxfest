Opening Conversation: Taking FOIA to Film

Friday, October 20, 2017 · 9:30 AM–10:15 AM · National Union Building

Public records & FOIA

For journalists, the Freedom of Information Act is a familiar tool for unlocking government documents and data, often in massive quantities. For filmmakers, however, the challenge is rendering the volume and banality of data retrieved through FOIA requests into compelling cinema.
