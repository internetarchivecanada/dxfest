Safety on the Frontlines

Wednesday, October 14, 2020 · 12:30pm–1:45pm

Legal & safety for reporters

From covering protests, to communicating with vulnerable sources, to investigating wrongdoing in dangerous environments – all during a global pandemic – journalists and filmmakers are facing unique and unprecedented threats to themselves and their ability to tell important stories. This workshop will provide practical, hands-on guidance designed to help journalists, including documentarians, understand their legal rights, protect their physical safety, and shore up their digital security at a time of heightened risk. Led by Harlo Holmes (Freedom of the Press Foundation), Elisa Muñoz (International Women’s Media Foundation), and Katie Townsend (Reporters Committee for Freedom of the Press), the workshop will provide advice and best practices devote special attention to helping journalists be prepared to for safely covering protests and other demonstrations, including tips for securing their digital devices and avoiding physical danger and arrest.
