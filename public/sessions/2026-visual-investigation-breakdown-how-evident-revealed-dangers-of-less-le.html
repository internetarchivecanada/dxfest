Visual Investigation Breakdown: How Evident revealed dangers of ‘less-lethal’ weaponry (and you can too)

Saturday, November 7, 2026 · 11:30am–12:45pm · The Johns Hopkins University Bloomberg Center

Investigative methods

The nonprofit newsroom Evident teams up with the Human Rights Center at UC Berkeley to present their visual investigation into a controversial weapon used by police on the streets of American cities. This session will pull back the curtain on the research and storytelling process, featuring documentary storytelling, data visualization, and a range of dynamic visual journalism. The team behind the project will also offer guidance and ideas for citizen journalism to cover police use of less-lethal weaponry.
