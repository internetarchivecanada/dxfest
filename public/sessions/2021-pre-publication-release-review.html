PRE-PUBLICATION/RELEASE REVIEW

Friday, October 15, 2021 · 9:45am–11:15am

Legal & safety for reporters

Documentary filmmakers need legal assistance to bring their journalistic and artistic visions to their audiences. Like other journalists, they must comply with laws relating to newsgathering, seek information through sunshine laws and carefully avoid defamation and privacy issues. This workshop will cover these legal issues and explain the pre-publication review or “vetting” process, which aims to lower the film’s legal risk, focusing on key takeaways for documentary filmmakers. In addition, other journalists, including podcasters, are welcome to attend, as these vetting principles also apply to their work.
