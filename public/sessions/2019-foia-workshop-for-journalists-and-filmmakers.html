FOIA Workshop for Journalists and Filmmakers

Saturday, October 12, 2019 · 10:00 AM–11:30 AM · Crystal Room, Eaton Hotel

Public records & FOIA

Federal, state, and local governments possess a wealth of information that can be of great use to journalists, including documentary filmmakers, but obtaining that material under state and federal public records laws isn’t always easy. This workshop led by media attorneys from the Reporters Committee for Freedom of the Press will help you learn how to successfully navigate FOI laws, from crafting strong public records requests to overcoming denials. Presented in collaboration with The Reporters Committee for Freedom of the Press, International Documentary Association, and the Hollywood Foreign Press Association.
