Panel · Double Exposure 2015

A Case of Identity

Thursday, October 1, 2015 · 10:30 AM–12:00 PM · Woolly Mammoth Theatre, Main Theatre

Documentary craft

Investigative journalist… Filmmaker… Visual storyteller… What do each of these identities mean for the way stories are told, for the role of the storyteller, for ethics and legal protections? As the boundaries between these crafts blur and collide, are the distinct identities vanishing as well, or are they more important than ever?
