Working with Participants in Investigative Film: A Conversation with Raoul Peck

Friday, November 8, 2024 · 3:50 PM–5:00 PM · The Johns Hopkins University Bloomberg Center

Natalie Bullock Brown, director of the Documentary Accountability Working Group (DAWG), engages in a conversation with filmmaker Raoul Peck, focusing on his work with participants in his documentary Silver Dollar Road. In this discussion, she applies the DAWG framework to explore the nuances of investigative filmmaking and the responsibilities filmmakers have toward their subjects.
