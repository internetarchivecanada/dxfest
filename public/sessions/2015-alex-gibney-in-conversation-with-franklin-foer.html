Conversation · Double Exposure 2015

Alex Gibney, in Conversation with Franklin Foer

Friday, October 2, 2015 · 1:30 PM–3:00 PM · Newseum, Knight Conference Center, Room B

Corruption & powerWomen, gender & sexualityDocumentary craftReligion

Award-winning filmmaker Alex Gibney has long bridged investigative journalism and filmmaking. He has tackled some of the most topical stories of our day, including political and corporate corruption, deception by sports figures, and sexual abuse in the Catholic church. His film Going Clear: Scientology and the Prison of Belief (2015) recently won three Emmys. Franklin Foer, an author and fellow at the New America Foundation, is a former editor of The New Republic.
