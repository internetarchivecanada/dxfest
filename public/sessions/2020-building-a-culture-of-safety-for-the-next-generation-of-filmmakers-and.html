Building a Culture of Safety for the Next Generation of Filmmakers and Journalists

Wednesday, October 14, 2020 · 12:30pm–1:45pm

War, conflict & the militaryDemocracy & electionsMiddle East & South Asia

The James W. Foley Legacy Foundation (JWFLF) was started in 2014 after the murder of James Foley, an American freelance journalist, while reporting in Syria. Over the past six years, JWFLF has strived to foster a culture of safety among aspiring journalists through its safety curricula. Diane Foley, founder and president, and Tom Durkin, education program director, will share highlights from the James W. Foley Journalism Safety Modules. This discussion will focus on covering protests, reporting during the pandemic, understanding journalists’ rights, the specific targeting of journalists of color and female journalists, and the need for diversity in newsrooms.
