The Mother Trap

Directed by Mark Helenowski

USA · 2023 · 17 min · Short

151617181920212223242526
Criminal justice & prisons

About the film

“Failure-to-protect” laws incarcerate women across the country for child abuse perpetrated by their intimate partners. In this unsparing mini-documentary, filmmaker Mark Helenowski teamed up with Mother Jones reporter Samantha Michaels to expose an appalling injustice that disproportionately hurts Black moms, including an Oklahoma mother of four, Kerry King, who is serving 30 years in a state prison—12 years longer than the man who abused her and her daughter.
