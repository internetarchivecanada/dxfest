What makes a person risk everything to bring out evidence, when powerful interests would rather it disappeared? In Steven Leckart's An Unknown Substance, Evgenia Kara-Murza becomes the public voice of her husband, Russian opposition figure Vladimir Kara-Murza, after he is sentenced to 25 years for speaking out against the war in Ukraine.
