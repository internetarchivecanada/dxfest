About the film

CLOSING NIGHT: An American family becomes entangled in a bizarre web of secrets and lies when their son is targeted by the U.S. government.

Enemies of the State

investigates the strange case of Matt DeHart, an alleged hacker and whistleblower, and his former Cold War spy parents who believe they are at the center of a government conspiracy and are ready to do anything to save their son from prison. A live post screening discussion with filmmaker Sonia Kennebeck, producer Ines Hofmann Kanna and protagonist Gabriella Coleman, moderated by Tetiana Anderson, will be available in addition to the film.
