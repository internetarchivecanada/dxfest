About the film

During his childhood in the early 80s, Gerad Argeros fell victim to the Reverend James Brzyski, the most prolific known abuser in the Archdiocese of the Philadelphia Catholic Church. In 2017, the first in a series of tragic deaths of Brzyski's other victims from the neighborhood made it clear the time had come to tell the survivor story of Fox Chase Boy.
