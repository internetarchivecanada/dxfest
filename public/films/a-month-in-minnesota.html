Short Strand: Minneapolis: On the Record + A Special Screening

November 7, 2026

, 3:00 PM

MLK Jr. Memorial Library

A Month in Minnesota, directed by Anne Sundberg, captures a city on edge, whose residents refused to look the other way when masked immigration agents turned up by the hundreds and then thousands. At one pitched moment, an ICE agent throws his hand before a camera filming an injured man curled on the ground.

"Do not block my camera!" an unseen young man shouts at the agent. "It is our one right, and you know that!"

This short strand will be followed by a Q&A with director Anne Sundberg.
