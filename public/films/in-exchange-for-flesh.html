About the film

Co-directed by Corey Devon Arthur and Sandro Ramani, In Exchange For Flesh is a searing 15-minute exposé that uses testimonial from prison, state archival footage, and art to confront the strip frisk: a brutal daily ritual that blurs the line between safety and sexual abuse.
