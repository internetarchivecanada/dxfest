About the film

Hailed a miracle worker, Las Vegas fertility doctor Dr. Quincy Fortier practiced for decades. Now, with the advent of commercial DNA testing, his secret is out. Dr. Fortier covertly inseminated his patients with his own sperm, without their knowledge or consent.

Baby God

follows the men and women who must grapple with the shattering discovery that Dr. Fortier is their biological father. As they seek the truth about the doctor's motives, they must also attempt to reshape their own identities. A pre-recorded, post screening discussion with filmmaker Hannah Olson, moderated by Lizzie Presser, will be available in addition to the film.
