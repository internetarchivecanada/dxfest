About the film

November 7, 2026

, 9:00 PM

U.S. Navy Memorial

Three films about the intimate ties that hold us to each other, and what those bonds cost. In Surrendered, Marie-Josée Desharnais weaves archival fragments, repetition and sound into a meditation on pressure, memory and survival. In Casa Mia, BAFTA-winning journalist and director Alexandra Genova moves past the familiar mythology of the mafia to the women of Naples’ Camorra, who live in the space between love and fear, rarely shown with this much complexity on screen. Artificial Love, winner of a 2026 Student Academy Award, is Arjun Fischer's sensitive portrait of a lonely man in frictionless love.

Screenings followed by Q&A with Marie-Josée Desharnais, Alexandra Catherine Genova, and Arjun Fischer, moderated by Diana Jean Schemo.

SURRENDERED

SURRENDERED is an experimental short film exploring psychological pressure, dissociation, memory, and survival through a non-narrative, sensory approach. The film works through fragmented imagery, repetition, restrained narration, archival material, and sound rather than conventional storytelling.

Director: Marie-Josée Desharnais

CASA MIA

Casa Mia places women at the heart of a world shaped by male violence, exploring the fraught liminal space between love and fear. Through a deeply intimate and unflinching portrait, it reveals the brutal, messy complexities of women’s role in the Neapolitan Camorra and their poignant journeys of personal redemption.

Director: Alexandra Catherine Genova

ARTIFICIAL LOVE

A lonely man falls in love with his ChatGPT companion, challenging the boundaries of love and what it means to be human.

Director: Arjun Fischer
