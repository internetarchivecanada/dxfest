Killing Anna

Directed by Sam Benstead

About the film

U.S. Premiere

November 7, 2026

, 12:00 PM

U.S. Navy Memorial

Living in Amsterdam, far from the war in Syria, academic Annsar Shahhoud receives a video no one was meant to see: an army unit massacring civilians in Tadamon, a suburb of Damascus. The main killer's face is visible. His name is not.

To find him, Shahhoud invents "Anna," a sympathetic stranger online, and spends month after month befriending men inside the regime's security apparatus, one guarded conversation at a time. She records what she learns and preserves every scrap, though no court is waiting and no one can promise the evidence will ever be used. A single slip could end everything.

Sam Benstead's tense, intimate film tracks the cost of that persistence as the line between Annsar and Anna begins to blur. It is a portrait of documentation as an act of faith: building the record now, in the hope that someday, justice may call for it.

Screening followed by a Q&A with director Sam Benstead, moderated by Ron Nixon, former director of the Associated Press Local Investigative Journalism Program and 100Reporters board member.
