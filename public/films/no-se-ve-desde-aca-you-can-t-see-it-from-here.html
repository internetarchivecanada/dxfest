Directed by Enrique Pedráza-Botero

151617181920212223242526

About the film

No se ve desde acá documents immigrant communities in Miami caught between arrival and belonging. Through observational vignettes and archival footage dating to the 1930s, it explores American individualism and shifting Latin American politics, beginning with Gustavo Petro's historic presidency and its impact on immigrant identity and opportunity.
