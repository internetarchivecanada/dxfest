About the film

A political thriller set in the murky world of post-war Guatemala,

The Art of Political Murder

uncovers the truth behind the brutal murder of Bishop Juan Gerardi, killed days after presenting a damning report implicating Guatemala’s military in the atrocities of the Civil War. A team of brave, young investigators - assembled by the Church - unearth a web of conspiracy and murder, but as they edge closer to the truth, will the country’s criminal underbelly succeed in silencing them? A pre-recorded, post screening discussion with filmmaker Paul Taylor and

The Art of Political Murder

author Francisco Goldman, moderated by Daniel Wilkinson, will be available in addition to the film.
