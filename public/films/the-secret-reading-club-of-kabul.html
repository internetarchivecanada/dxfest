The Secret Reading Club of Kabul

Directed by Shakiba Adil, Elina Hirvonen

About the film

D.C. Premiere

November 8, 2026

, 2:35 PM

U.S. Navy Memorial

Five years after the Taliban returned to power, Afghan women are barred from secondary school, university and much of public life. In Kabul, a small group of young women meets anyway, in secret, to do something now forbidden to them: read together.

Filmed from inside that circle, where discovery could cost them everything, Shakiba Adil and Elina Hirvonen’s film captures what headlines cannot. Careers and studies abruptly cut off. Friendships that become lifelines. The quiet defiance of turning a page.

In

The Diary of Anne Frank

, written by a Jewish girl in hiding who did not survive the Holocaust, they find echoes of their own sudden isolation and deferred dreams. Inspired by Anne, they begin keeping their own diaries and filming their lives indoors and out as an act of witness: a way to preserve voices the authorities have worked hard to silence.

Screening followed by a Q&A with film editor Annukka Lilja and film participant Rukhshana, moderated by Joanne Leedom-Ackerman, novelist and journalist.
