Who Is Living in My Father’s House?

Directed by Isaac George Crown Manesis

USA · 2023 · 10 min · Short

151617181920212223242526

About the film

My family left Greece throughout the early and mid-20th century, forgetting their homes to become Americans. In the process of forgetting a lost home, a house was stolen. I went to Erikousa to get to the bottom of this nefarious plot, make a film chronicling my journey, and reclaim my family’s house. However, the nefarious plot had no bottom and my camera had dust on it. This film is about stories that don’t fit the confines of either fiction or nonfiction.
