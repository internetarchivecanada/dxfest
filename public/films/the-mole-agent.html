About the film

When a family grows concerned for their mother’s well-being in a retirement home, private investigator Romulo hires 83-year-old Sergio to pose as a new resident and undercover spy inside the facility.

The Mole Agent

follows Sergio’s struggle with his cloak-and-dagger assignment as his involvement with the aging residents deepens. A pre-recorded, post screening discussion with filmmaker Maite Alberdi, moderated by Sky Sitney, will be available in addition to the film.
