Short Strand: Minneapolis: On the Record + A Special Screening

November 7, 2026

, 3:00 PM

MLK Jr. Memorial Library

When hundreds and then thousands of masked immigration agents descended on Minneapolis in the winter of 2025, residents put on their boots and picked up their cameras.

They’re Here

, from Oscar-winning filmmaker Laura Poitras and Rachel Mueller, is built from footage shot by local activists who tracked ICE operations and the challenges to them across the city.

This short strand will be followed by a Q&A with Minneapolis-based producer Adam Mellion.
