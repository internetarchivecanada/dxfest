About the film

Despite focusing on themes of espionage and terrorism, The Accidental Spy is essentially a love story centered around a family of New Yorkers who never gave up hope. Like so many immigrants before him, Blerim Skoro believed in the power of the American dream. He had moved mountains to bring his family to the country, albeit breaking the law along the way, but now had to suffer the consequences of his actions by being deported back to Kosovo. But rather than be separated from his wife, children, and extended family, he chose to find a way to get back home.
