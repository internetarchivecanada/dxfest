About the film

Cover-Up is a riveting odyssey through the fascinating career of Seymour Hersh—whose relentless investigative reporting exposed some of America's most explosive official secrets, from the My Lai massacre to the CIA's darkest operations. Directed by Oscar- winner Laura Poitras and Emmy-winner Mark Obenhaus, the film follows Hersh as he reopens his stacks of personal notes, documents, and memories, tracing 50 years spent confronting the U.S. military, intelligence, and media's capacity for concealment and spin.

Blending interviews, archival footage, and Hersh's own reflections, Cover-Up not only tells the story of a legendary outsider, but probes the ethical and personal risks of a lifetime of adversarial journalism.
