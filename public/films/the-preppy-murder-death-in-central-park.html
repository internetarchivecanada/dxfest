The Preppy Murder: Death in Central Park

Directed by Ricki Stern, Annie Sundberg

USA · 2019 · 86 min

151617181920212223242526

About the film

A five-part investigative documentary that revisits the infamous murder of Jennifer Levin at the hands of her prep school friend Robert Chambers in Central Park in the summer of 1986. The case became a tabloid sensation and sparked a national conversation on female sexuality, power and privilege. Award winning filmmakers Ricki Stern and Annie Sundberg take a fresh look at the case, meticulously blending a trove of archival material with contemporary interviews, in a retrospective glance marked by haunting new perspective on the case that shook a city, if not a nation.
