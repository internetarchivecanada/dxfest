About the film

Teaching America is a short documentary exploring the battle around the teaching of African-American history and studies from inside the classroom, focusing on the transformative journeys of the Arkansas teachers, students and families who are part of the inaugural classes of students taking Advanced Placement African-American Studies nationwide.
