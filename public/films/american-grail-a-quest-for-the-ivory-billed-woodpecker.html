USA · 2024 · 16 min · Short

About the film

The siren call of the ivory-billed woodpecker lures a New York-based tantric sex teacher into the swamps of the American South on a 17-year journey to prove the mythic bird still exists. Along the way, he finds a tendril of hope in the face of climate change.
