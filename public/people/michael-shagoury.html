Biography

Michael Shagoury is digital media professional and entrepreneur who is currently executive producer and acting editorial lead of AJ+. He manages a group of media professionals that are leading the way in digital storytelling on social platforms. As a broadcast professional, Michael produced news and documentaries for a wide range of international news channels including CNN, Al-Jazeera, BBC, ZDF and NBC. In addition to journalism and content creation, Michael spearheaded the establishment of World Media Academy in Dubai, which trains professionals in skills related to broadcasting, print journalism, public relations and new media.
