Biography

Lindsay Poulton is Head of Documentaries at The Guardian where she commissions and curates the Guardian‘s documentary films. Her core focus is the documentary strand for short films with character-led narratives that are about compelling, contemporary stories, including the Oscar-winning Colette. Lindsay is passionate about innovation in digital storytelling and has a strong journalistic as well as filmmaking background. Her work has been shown at festivals around the world, including Sundance, Tribeca, IDFA, Cannes, and has been recognized with a number of prestigious awards.
