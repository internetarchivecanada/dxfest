Biography

Alisia Essig graduated in Communications and Political Science from the University of Utah and completed her Masters degree in Public Administration at The George Washington University. Alisia worked for Congressman Jason Chaffetz (R-UT) from 2008-2011 as his Communications Director and Legislative Aide. She managed and coordinated the Congressman’s overall press strategy as well as formulated health care related legislative proposals and policy developments. Since 2011 Alisia has worked for Mia Love’s Congressional Campaign in Utah’s fourth district. Alisia managed all local and national media requests as Love’s Press Secretary during both her 2012 and 2014 campaign.
