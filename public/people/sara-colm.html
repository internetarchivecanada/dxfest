Biography

Sara Colm is a specialist on human rights issues in Cambodia and Vietnam with more than 20 years’ experience in Southeast Asia. From 1992-2011 she was based in Cambodia, where she helped establish the Phnom Penh Post as its first managing editor. She subsequently worked as a human rights officer for the U.N. Transitional Authority in Cambodia and as advisor to Cambodian and international human rights groups. From 1998-2011 she headed up Human Rights Watch’s work on Cambodia and Vietnam as its Phnom Penh-based representative. Prior to moving to Cambodia, her work as editor-in-chief of the Tenderloin Times, a community newspaper for Southeast Asians in San Francisco, was nominated for a Pulitzer Prize. She is currently working as an independent research consultant for nonprofit organizations and writing a book documenting the history of Cambodia’s indigenous minorities during the Khmer Rouge regime.
