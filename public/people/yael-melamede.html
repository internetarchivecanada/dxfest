Biography

Yael Melamede is the co-founder of SALTY Features, an independent production company based in New York City. Melamede’s most recent film and directorial debut is (DIS)HONESTY: THE TRUTH ABOUT LIES, based on the work of behavioral scientist Dan Ariely. Melamede’s producing credits include INOCENTE, winner of the 2013 Academy Award for Best Documentary Short.
