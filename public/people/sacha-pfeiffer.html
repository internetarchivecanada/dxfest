Biography

A print and broadcast journalist, Sacha Pfeiffer was a member of the Boston Globe Spotlight Team that won the 2003 Pulitzer Prize for Public Service for its stories on clergy sex abuse in the Roman Catholic Church. She has also been the host of All Things Considered and Radio Boston at WBUR, Boston’s NPR station, where she won a national 2012 Edward R. Murrow Award for broadcast reporting, and a guest host of NPR’s nationally syndicated On Point and Here & Now. Pfeiffer currently writes about wealth, philanthropy and nonprofits for the Globe, where she has also covered legal affairs and the state courts, produced numerous other investigative series, and done a lively range of reporting and writing, from magazine stories to travel pieces to restaurant reviews. At WBUR and NPR, in addition to show hosting, Pfeiffer anchored election coverage, debates, political panels and other special events. She came to radio as a senior reporter covering health, science, medicine and the environment, and her on-air work received numerous other awards from the Radi o and Television News Directors Association and the Associated Press. Pfeiffer was a 2004-5 John S. Knight journalism fellow at Stanford University, where she studied at Stanford Law School. She is a co-author of Betrayal: The Crisis in the Catholic Church and has been an adjunct faculty member at Boston University’s College of Communication.
