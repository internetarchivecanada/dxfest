Biography

Michael Fearon is a documentary filmmaker from Northern California. His work focuses on people engaging with the natural world. He has produced films about a young Sherpa in Nepal, a mezcal distillery in rural Oaxaca, and a school for autistic children. Previously he worked at the Council on Foreign Relations. He is now an associate producer at Blue Chalk Media.
