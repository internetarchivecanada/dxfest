Biography

Joshua Altman is an American film director, producer, writer, and an award-winning director of photography and editor. He makes his directorial debut with All These Sons, and was the editor of Minding the Gap along with numerous critically acclaimed films, including two Sundance Grand Jury Prize winners. Among his credits are The Tillman Story, The Final Year and We Live in Public.
