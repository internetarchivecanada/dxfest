    Biography

    Adam Villani (Director, Chasing Westboro) is a Chicago-based documentary filmmaker and artist from the suburbs of Washington, DC. He is a recent graduate of Columbia College Chicago where he received a degree in Cinema Art and Science with a concentration in documentary film. He freelances as a director and DP for documentaries, commercials, music videos, and corporate films. His own documentaries include Bad Guys (which explores the stigmas placed on people that feel forced to rely on crime as a source of income or protection); Scum (about a moral person who documents his journey casting himself as a manipulative person that dates multiple people at the same time); and Chasing Westboro (a personal documentary chronicling his journey  of reckoning with the Westboro Baptist Church).
