Biography

Chris Fenton served as president of DMG Entertainment Motion Picture Group and GM of DMG North American for 17 years, internally orchestrating the creative and business activities of DMG: a multi-billion dollar global media company headquartered in Beijing. He has produced or supervised 21 films, grossing $2 billion in worldwide box offices. As an author, Fenton chronicled much of that work in Feeding the Dragon: Inside the Trillion Dollar Dilemma Facing Hollywood, the NBA, & American Business.

At present, he speaks regularly as a China expert and serves as CEO of Media Capital Technologies, having concluded a successful term as Senior Advisor to IDW Media Holdings, focused on streamlining operations, expanding international business, and restructuring investments. Fenton also hosts U.S. Congressional member delegations on diplomatic missions to China focused on trade, media, and investment. He is a Trustee of the U.S.-Asia Institute and services on several company boards. Fenton is a contributor for RealClearPolitics, The Federalist, and The Wrap, and regularly appears on Bloomberg, Tucker Carlson, Fox Business, BBC, CNBC, NPR, Adam Carolla, War Room, and VOA.

Fenton, who holds a B.S. in Engineering from Cornell University, resides with his wife and two children in Manhattan Beach, CA.

Chris will be presenting on the Censored in Beijing panel.
