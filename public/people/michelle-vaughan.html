Biography

Michelle Vaughan grew up surrounded by art. She spent 3 years in a traditional program until moving to a conceptual one at UCLA. That education shaped her approach to art and she got to study under many accomplished artists. After graduating, Michelle took more technical art classes and continues to learn new skills when necessary. She is a project-based artist primarily making drawings and prints but will expand into various mediums and sometimes collaborate with other artists. Topics include politics, history and art history, sociology, science and occasionally food. Michelle lives with her husband, Felix Salmon, in a Brutalist tower in Chinatown, NYC.

Michelle Vaughan will be a participant on the DEFY APATHY! Workshop to Build Public Trust in Journalism workshop.
