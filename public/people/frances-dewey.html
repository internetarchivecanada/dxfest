    Biography

    Frances Dewey is the Festival Manager of Double Exposure Investigative Film Festival and Symposium. She is a recent graduate of Georgetown University, where she studied Classics and Film and Media.

    Frances has experience in entertainment and media. She has worked as a production assistant at independent documentary film company 50 Eggs Films (A Most Beautiful Thing, I Am Jane Doe) and interned in the Content Acquisitions department at television network Showtime.

    Frances’ study of Film and Media at Georgetown culminated in her co-creating a serialized historical fiction podcast based on the Salem Witch Trials. Frances has also completed production coursework at USC School of Cinematic Arts.

    On campus Frances served as the president of the Georgetown Independent Film Society, an organization with which she was involved since her freshman year. She was also a member of the Multimedia department at The Hoya, the university’s oldest student-run newspaper; she was responsible for creating original digital content for its online platforms.

    Frances had the privilege of volunteering at Double Exposure in 2017 and is thrilled to be involved with the festival once again. She is currently based in New York City, where she was born and raised.
