    Biography

    Mark J. Rochester is editor in chief of Type Investigations. He was previously senior news director for investigations at the Detroit Free Press. His career includes other senior leadership positions at the Indianapolis Star, Newsday, the Denver Post, the Associated Press and the Pittsburgh Post-Gazette. He served on the national board of directors of Investigative Reporters & Editors Inc. and is currently on the national advisory board of the Investigative Reporting Workshop at American University in Washington, D.C.

    Mark will be moderating the Playbook for a Pandemic: Disinformation in the Age of COVID-19 panel.
