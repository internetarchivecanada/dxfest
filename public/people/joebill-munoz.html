Biography

JoeBill Muñoz is a Mexican-American documentary filmmaker and director and producer of The Strike (Independent Lens). He has directed short films for Independent Lens (Maletero and Evidence Lost) and NBC (Follow the Sun), and produced feature films and television series, including The Grab (Hulu), The Circus (Showtime), The New York Times Presents (Hulu) and more. His work has been supported through fellowships from the Sundance Institute, New America, Firelight Media, SFFILM and Dart Center for Journalism and Trauma. Originally from Texas, he resides in New York City.
