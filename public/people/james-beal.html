Biography

James Beal is the deputy investigations editor for The Times. Previously social affairs editor, he joined The Times after a six-year stint in New York as the US editor of The Sun. Before that, James worked as a general news reporter for The Sun in London and as a reporter for SWNS news agency in Bristol.
