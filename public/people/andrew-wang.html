Biography

Andrew Wang is a freelance journalist covering the NFT space. In his writing he is most fascinated by how NFTs can bridge artists with communities to build social capital in potentially novel ways. A recent graduate school dropout, he lives in New York City, where the metaverse is as accessible as anywhere else.
