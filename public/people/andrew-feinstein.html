Biography

Andrew Feinstein is a former ANC Member of Parliament from South Africa. He served under Nelson Mandela before resigning in protest at Mandela’s successor’s refusal to allow an unfettered investigation into a massive arms deal tainted by allegations of high level corruption. He is the author of ‘After the Party: Corruption, the ANC and South Africa’s Uncertain Future’ (Verso, 2009) and ‘The Shadow World: Inside the Global Arms Trade’ (Farrar Straus & Giroux, 2011). He is the writer on the feature documentary of ‘Shadow World’ which will premiere in early 2016, directed by Johan Grimonprez and produced by Louverture Films. Andrew is Executive Director of the London-based organisation Corruption Watch, which investigates and documents the impact of bribery and corruption on democracy, governance and development. Andrew has written for, amongst others, the Guardian, the New York Times, the New Statesman and Der Spiegel. He appears regularly on the BBC, CNN, Al Jazeera, Democracy Now and many others. He was named as one of the world’s 100 most influential people working against armed violence and was voted South Africa’s ‘Anti-corruption Hero of 2014’. Feinstein lives in London with his wife and two children.
