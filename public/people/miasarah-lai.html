    Biography

    Miasarah Lai is an independent documentary filmmaker and freelancer based in Chicago. From the Lower East Side, New York City, Lai’s Puerto Rican-Chinese heritage and diverse neighborhood shaped her global interests in visualizing alternative female and minority narratives. Her work has screened at film festivals including Athens Ethnofest, Human Rights Human Dignity International Film Festival, Wathann Film Festival, American Documentary Film Festival, Los Angeles Asian Pacific Film Festival, New Orleans Film Festival and Big Sky Film Festival. When she is not working on her own projects, Lai organizes two feminist collectives: Ethnocine, an ethnographic filmmaking collective, and Mezcla Media Collective, a hub for filmmakers of color in Chicago.
