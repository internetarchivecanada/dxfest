    Biography

    Christoph Jörg is an independent documentary producer and Story Consultant with thirty years of experience. As the founder of Paris-based Vestigo Films (2022) and Pumpernickel Films (2009), and the Managing Director of Unchained Productions in New York, his recent works, include:

    An Eye for an Eye, The Oligarch and the Art Dealer, The Lost Leonardo, One Child Nation, Winnie, The Lovers and the Despot, Love Crimes of Kabul, Be Like Others, and As Far as They Can Run.

    Christoph worked as Senior Commissioning Editor for Arte France in Paris from 1992 to 2009, where he oversaw the conception and production of more than 300 documentaries. He is a member of the Producers Guild of America (PGA) and lives in New York.
