Biography

Chelsea Kelly counsels and defends clients in the media and technology industries, particularly involving defamation, copyright, trademark, right-of-publicity, newsgathering, and constitutional matters. An avid litigator, she has successfully argued before multiple courts, and has represented clients across the country.

Chelsea works to achieve the best possible results in litigation—whether that means winning a dispositive motion or taking the case to trial. She also assists clients in preventing litigation by reviewing and revising content prior to publication. Chelsea’s clients include major technology platforms, entertainment companies, news outlets, individual journalists, podcast producers, publishers, nonprofits, and advocacy organizations.
