Biography

Seung-ho Choi has worked as an investigative journalist since the 1990s. He has produced a number of investigative reports and directed two feature films. The first, Spy Nation, chronicles the South Korean intelligence agency’s history of falsely accusing North Korean defectors. The second, Criminal Conspiracy, focuses on a decade of presidential attacks against South Korea’s freedom of the press. Both films are now available on the Netflix.
