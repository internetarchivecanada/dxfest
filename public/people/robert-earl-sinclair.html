Biography

A graduate of LACHSA and NYU’s Tisch School of the Arts, Robert Sinclair is a future architect tinkering, building, and designing at the crossroads of art, culture, and technology with a dedication to beauty, justice, and inclusive imagination. Through his family creative practice at Sinclair Futures, and GoFA’s Futurist Writers Room, Robert’s art focuses on world building and storytelling aimed at inspiring the imagination of a future we actually want to live in.
