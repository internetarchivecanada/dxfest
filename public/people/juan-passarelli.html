Biography

Juan Luis Passarelli Sandhoff is a Guatemalan investigative journalist and documentary filmmaker based in Berlin. Director of The War on Journalism and The Engineer, he co-produced The Six Billion Dollar Man (Cannes 2025, Golden Globe winner). He has chronicled WikiLeaks and collaborated on dozens of documentaries.
