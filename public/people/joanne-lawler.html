    Biography

    Joanne Lawler is responsible, with Anabelle Rojas, for production management of the Documentary and Discussion Program strands in Washington, DC. She ensures smooth operation from pre-production through delivery within allocated budgets, and is responsible for the management of the Programming internship and fellowship programs. Lawler has performed a variety of roles over the past 25 years in the television broadcast industry: administrator/coordinator for AJE Technical Operations, managing contracts and budgets for AJE in the Americas; station relations representative for DeShields Associates, providing marketing and support for public television programs; and video editor, highlights including award winning pieces for the Eye on America series, CBS Evening News, ABC World News Tonight, Nightline and 20/20 as well as independent documentary and advocacy films.

    At Double Exposure, Joanne will hear pitches from filmmakers whose completed film is of global interest and would generate significant online discussion about the film and the deeper issues important to AJE’s online community.

    From the festival website (archived), 2021
