    Biography

    Lauren Durr is a native of Baton Rouge, Louisiana, and discovered a love for classic films and television at an early age. She pursued a degree at the Communications School of Elon University in North Carolina and graduated with a Bachelor of Arts in 2005. She began her career working as an editor at the CBS station, WRAL, in Raleigh, North Carolina. She would spend the next five years in Raleigh working in both news and a local production company, Drawbridge Media, specializing in producing and editing corporate video and reality television.

    Durr moved to New York in 2010 to pursue reality television editing. To date she has edited countless shows airing on networks including TLC, A&E, Discovery, HGTV, CNBC, and the Up Network. She specializes in character-driven shows that focus on relationships among many characters. In 2016, Durr directed and edited 1000 Year Flood, a feature investigative documentary focusing on the devastation of and recovery from Louisiana’s Great Flood. She currently lives in Los Angeles and continues to pursue a career in television.
