    Biography

    Justine Gubar serves as Senior Editor for the Sunday Long Read Newsletter, curating the Long View section that identifies top videos and films of the week.

    Until recently, Gubar was the Vice President of News Narratives for Fusion, the English-language cable network owned by Univision. In this role, she served as Executive Producer for the award winning investigative documentary series The Naked Truth. The series has been honored with multiple journalism awards including the RFK awards, NABJ, and a National Headliner Award. The first season of the series is now available on Netflix.

    In 2015, she authored the book Fanaticus: Mischief and Madness in the Modern Sports Fan which was published by Rowman and Littlefield. The book is based on much of her experience as a producer for ESPN. At ESPN for two decades, she covered a wide range of off-the-field issues including performance enhancing drug use, domestic violence and homophobia in sports.

    In 2011, she was honored with a Women in Cable Television (WICT) Signature Accolade award for Herstory: Ten Times Over, a documentary about basketball camps for girls in Nigeria. This project also received a NAMIC (National Association of Multi-Ethnicity in Communications) Vision award. For the academic year of 2006-7, she was awarded a John S. Knight Fellowship at Stanford University.

    A graduate of Wesleyan University, Gubar is a native New Yorker.
