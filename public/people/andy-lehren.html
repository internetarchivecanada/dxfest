    Biography

    Andy Lehren is senior editor for the NBC News investigative team and specializes in data journalism. Andy was previously at The New York Times. He led NBC’s work on the Implant Files, mined the Snowden files, and probed Wikileaks diplomatic cables troves, including for the best-selling book Open Secrets. He has covered the military, policing, terrorism, and more. Honors include a Polk, Peabody, Emmy, three IRE awards and contributing to a Pulitzer. He is Director of Investigative Reporting at CUNY’s Graduate School of Journalism.

    Andy will be moderating the Cross-Border Investigations panel.
