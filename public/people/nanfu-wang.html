Biography

2020 MacArthur Genius Grant recipient Nanfu Wang is an award-winning Chinese filmmaker based in the United States. Her feature documentaries include Hooligan Sparrow (Sundance 2016), I Am Another You (SXSW 2017), One Child Nation (Sundance 2019), and In the Same Breath (Sundance 2021). Wang is celebrated for her fearless, bold storytelling. She has received four Emmy® nominations, has been twice Academy Award shortlisted and is a recipient of the Independent Spirit Award, the Peabody Award, the George Polk Award, Cinema Eye Honor awards, and an IDA award.

Other bios (2)

Nanfu Wang is a Chinese filmmaker based in New York City. She directed and produced feature documentaries Hooligan Sparrow (Sundance 2016, shortlisted for the 2017 Academy Award for Best documentary feature), I Am Another You (SXSW 2017, Special Jury Prize winner), and One Child Nation (Sundance 2019, Grand Jury Prize Winner). Wang is the recipient of two Emmy nominations, an Independent Spirit Award, a Peabody Award, Cinema Eye Honor, George Polk Award, IDA award, and 20 other honors. Originally from a remote village in China, Wang overcame poverty and a lack of access to formal education en route to earning three master’s degrees from Shanghai University, Ohio University, and New York University. She teaches cinematography and editing at New York University.

Nanfu Wang will be a panelist on The Undertow panel.
