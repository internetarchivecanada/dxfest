Biography

Miriam Shor has worked as a director and actor in the film and television industry for over 25 years, and has focused on stories and characters that challenge traditional female narratives. She was recently nominated for an Emmy for her portrayal of HELEN in the AppleTV’s television show Pluribus. She directed and starred in the critically acclaimed television show YOUNGER on Paramount+/Netflix, earning a Critics Choice nomination for her work. She collaborated with director/actor John Cameron Mitchell on both the original off-Broadway production of HEDWIG AND THE ANGRY INCH as well as the subsequent groundbreaking feature film, creating the iconic role of “Yitzak”, (LA Outfest Award, Phoenix Critics Circle nom., Satellite Award nom.) Her work can be seen on AppleTV, HBO, FX, Netflix, ABC, CBS, NBC and many other platforms. Miriam is a proud union member of the Directors Guild of America, SAG-AFTRA and AEA.
