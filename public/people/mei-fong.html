    Biography

    Pulitzer-prize winning journalist and author Mei Fong covered Asia as a Wall Street Journal correspondent. Her first book, on China’s one-child policy, won a non-fiction award by the American Society of Journalists and Authors, was chosen as one of the top non-fiction books of 2016 by Zocalo and Medium and is called “a searing, important, and eminently readable exploration of China’s one-child policy,” by Nick Kristof in the New York Review of Books. She is a fellow at DC think tank New America and listed in Foreign Policy magazine as a Top 50 influencer on US-China relations. Fong was born in Malaysia and holds a bachelor’s degree from the National University of Singapore and a master’s in international relations from Columbia University.
