Biography

Kenyatta Cheese is a professional internet enthusiast. As co-founder of the audience development agency Everybody at Once, he gets creators to reframe the producer-consumer relationship as systems of participation. As co-creator of the internet meme database Know Your Meme, he has helped the web better understand itself as a vital space of emergent culture. In previous iterations he has worked with the Eyebeam Center for Art and Technology, Manhattan Neighborhood Network, and the online video network Rocketboom.
