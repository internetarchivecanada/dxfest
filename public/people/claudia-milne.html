Biography

Claudia Milne has more than 20 years experience in television and online journalism. At the BBC she led the launch of new television programs and developed new video formats on the web.

As a television producer, Milne worked around the world covering major news events and producing long-form pieces and investigations for the BBC’s Newsnight program. She has worked in the United States for more than 13 years, where she reported from New Orleans in the aftermath of Hurricane Katrina, and in 2012 she led the coverage of the U.S. election for BBC Online. More recently she relaunched Bloomberg Television’s daily programming. Her work has been recognized at the Emmys and the Peabodys.
