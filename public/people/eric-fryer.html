Biography

Eric Fryer is a dedicated political affairs professional with a passion for event execution and planning. In his current role as Manager, Grassroots and PAC at the U.S. Travel Association, Eric facilitates advocacy campaigns to increase grassroots involvement on key industry issues at the federal level. He also helps organize U.S. Travel’s annual legislative fly-in event (Destination Capitol Hill), the Travel Works for America campaign, and large-scale fundraising events.

Beyond his role at U.S. Travel, Eric has helped recruit volunteers and provided logistical support for various high-profile events, including the Concordia Annual Summit in New York City and nearly a dozen Wall Street Journal functions in Washington, DC. In 2023, he also led the planning and execution of a virtual town hall event series for a mayoral candidate’s campaign in Denver, CO.

Eric is thrilled to serve as the Volunteer Manager for the Double Exposure Film Festival this fall. His political background provides him with a unique perspective on organizing impactful events, which he hopes will enhance participation and engagement among attendees and volunteers.

Eric holds a B.A. in Political Science from American University, where he served as Class President during his junior and senior years. As resident of Washington, DC, he enjoys the city’s vibrant nightlife and theater scene.
