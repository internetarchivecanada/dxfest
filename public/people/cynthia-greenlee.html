    Biography

    Dr. Cynthia R. Greenlee is a North Carolina-based historian, writer, and editor. She holds a doctorate from Duke University and specializes in the legal and reproductive history of African-American girls and women. She is at work on a forthcoming book about the history of Black Americans and abortion from 1860 to more recent times. A noted expert on race and abortion, she appeared in a recent FRONTLINE short documentary about abortion opposition in Black communities. She is senior editor at Rewire.News, the leading publication devoted to covering reproductive health, rights and justice. Dr. Greenlee is also a member of Echoing Ida, a collective of Black women and non-binary people who write about reproductive justice.
