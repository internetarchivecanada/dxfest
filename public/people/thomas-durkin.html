Biography

Thomas Durkin is a longtime friend of journalist James Foley. He serves as the Education Program Director for The James W. Foley Legacy Foundation. In addition Tom is the Research & Grant Coordinator at Marquette University’s Center for Peacemaking as well as a Lecturer of English and Peace Studies.
