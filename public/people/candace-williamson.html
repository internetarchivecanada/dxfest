Biography

Candace Mae is an award-winning director and self-proclaimed ‘cool gal’ from Orlando, Florida. Known for her vibrant storytelling, her work is rooted in the belief that joy is a radical form of resistance. She views filmmaking as a powerful tool for fostering a sense of belonging.
