Biography

Beth Levison is an Emmy and Peabody Award-winning producer and director based in New York City. Her most recent producing efforts include Women in Blue (Tribeca Film Festival 2020/Independent Lens) and the Emmy-nominated film Made in Boise (AFI DOCS 2019/Independent Lens). Her previous producing credits include 32 Pills: My Sister’s Suicide (HBO 2017), the Emmy-nominated film Personal Statement (PBS 2018), and her independent directorial and producing debut, Lemon (PBS 2011). Beth is the founder of Hazel Pictures, a co-founder of the Documentary Producers Alliance (DPA), a member of AMPAS, and on the producing faculty at the School of Visual Arts (SVA).
