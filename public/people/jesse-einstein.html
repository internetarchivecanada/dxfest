Biography

Jesse Einstein has produced, directed, written and acted in internationally distributed content with partners such as Concordia Studio, Boardwalk Pictures, Jubilee Media, Radical Productions and Sky TV. He has produced two feature documentaries and directed multiple narrative shorts. He has also directed and produced audio experiences for Audible Studios. Jesse is currently adapting some of his favorite musicals into films.
