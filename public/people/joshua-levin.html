Biography

Josh Levin is the head of Sales and Acquisitions at Passion River Films, and the former general manager of West End Cinema in Washington, DC.  He has been a film distribution executive, documentary film producer and art house cinema operator, as well as a professor of film distribution, marketing and finance at New York University and American University.

Josh is a former Chief Marketing Officer at Film Movement, the New York-based art house cinema distributor, where he created and managed marketing, advertising and publicity campaigns for over 25 feature films released to the theatrical, festival, institutional, home video and television markets. He ha worked on films featuring Philip Seymour Hoffman, Elliot Page, Sandra Oh and Rose Byrne, and from top award-nominated directors Deepa Mehta, Joseph Cedar and Scott Hamilton Kennedy.

As a filmmaker, Josh has produced or executive produced five documentary features, including CORMAN’S WORLD: Exploits of a Hollywood Rebel (Sundance and Cannes, 2011, national theatrical release, broadcast by A&E) and CAPE SPIN: An American Power Struggle (IDFA 2011, Film at Lincoln Center Art of the Real November 2012, platform theatrical release, broadcast by Link TV).

Other bios (1)

Joshua Levin is the co-founder of West End Cinema, which featured independent, foreign and documentary films, and served as general manager of the venue for several years.

In 2013, Josh launched a separate nonprofit called the West End Cinema Education Initiative (WECEI), which he operates as executive director. WECEI offers free screenings of age-appropriate, educationally valuable films to DC public and charter school classes through a program called WE SEE FILMS.

Josh also produces documentary films, including “Corman’s World: Exploits of a Hollywood Rebel” (Sundance and Cannes, 2011); “Cape Spin: An American Power Struggle” (IDFA 2011, Film at Lincoln Center Art of the Real November 2012); and “Demon On Wheels” (in production). Functions includes funding, fundraising, drafting marketing plans and pitches, finding strategic promotional partners, rights clearances, and more.
