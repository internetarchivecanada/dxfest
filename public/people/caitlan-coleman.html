Biography

Caitlan Coleman was taken hostage by the Haqqani Network in October of 2012, and held for five years and one day, until 2017. Since then, Caitlan and her children have settled in Virginia. Caitlan has since endeavored to share her experience and lend what advice and advocacy she can to educate and help on hostage policy, with hostage families, and the reintegration needs of former hostages and illegal detainees. She has recently begun working with the JWFLF on their education modules for journalists, specifically around mitigating risks to prevent and survive captivity overseas.
