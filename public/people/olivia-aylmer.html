Biography

Olivia (she/her) is an independent producer and arts & culture writer. Whether producing podcasts and events or publishing zines, she is drawn to multimedia projects that catalyze people to experience our world with more curiosity and connectivity. In addition to producing Viewers Like Us in 2021, podcast credits include Lit Up (Editorial & Marketing Consultant); The Tip Off (Producer, 9th season); and Crossing the Line (Story Producer). Olivia’s writing spanning the worlds of film, books, style, and culture has been published by vanityfair.com, Wine Zine, Lindsay, Vestoj, and AnOther, among other outlets.

Most recently, she worked with AIR (Association of Independents in Radio), where she helped tell the organization’s story to partners, funders, and the greater public as they expanded their work in support of independent audio makers. Previously, she held staff roles at Vanity Fair and Little, Brown. She is also the co-founder of Constellation, a digital platform created in 2017 to amplify emerging and established creatives of marginalized genders across the globe. Olivia organizes alongside fellow media workers to improve labor conditions across the industry with the Freelance Solidarity Project, a division of the National Writers Union.

Raised in Queens and based in Brooklyn, she is a 2015 graduate of Barnard College.
