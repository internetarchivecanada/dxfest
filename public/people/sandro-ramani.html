Biography

Sandro Ramani is a Brooklyn-based documentary filmmaker whose work exposes hidden systems of power, including the American prison system. Designed to drive social and legislative change, his films have screened in advocacy settings such as The People’s Forum, The Center for Art and Advocacy, and law schools nationwide.
