Biography

Chris is a documentary filmmaker and photographer with over 14 years’ experience working as a film and television director, editor, producer and cameraman. He has spent the last six years living in Cambodia filming the feature documentary ‘The Cause of Progress’, which is in post production now. His first film, ‘GuinnessSize Me’, was shown in over 20 festivals around the world and won ‘Best International Documentary’ at the 2005 Atlanta Documentary Festival. His most recent film, Super- market Slave Trail, which was produced for the Guardian, had a major impact on the global retail and fishing industries, one result being that many supermarkets across the world no longer sell prawns farmed in Thailand. The film was seen at the highest levels of the US government and was influential in downgrading Thailand to the lowest rung of the State Department’s 2014 human trafficking Index, which measures a nation’s willingness and efforts to combat slavery within its borders. Chris is the founder of production company Little Ease Films Ltd.
