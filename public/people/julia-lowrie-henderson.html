Biography

Julia Lowrie Henderson is a graduate of the Salt Institute for Documentary Studies. Her radio work has been featured on Studio 360, Maine Public Broadcasting Network, New Hampshire Public Radio, Public Radio Remix, Sirius XM, ESPN, and more. She is a founding producer of ESPN’s 30 for 30 Podcasts, and has produced both of their breakout serialized seasons: Bikram and The Sterling Affairs. Her work for ESPN has been nominated for an IDA Award for Best Audio Documentary. Prior to that, she was an associate producer at WNYC’s Studio 360, where she was awarded a 2016 New York Press Club Award. She holds a BFA in theatre from Boston University. She is a radio producer, documentarian, storyteller, actor, and playwright.

Julia will be moderating the post-screening discussion of BIKRAM: YOGI GURU PREDATOR
