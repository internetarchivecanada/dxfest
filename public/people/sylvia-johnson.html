Biography

Sylvia Johnson is a filmmaker and impact strategist. She currently splits her time as Creative Director between Free Roaming Studios, a small production company dedicated to visual storytelling to inspire action, and the Santa Fe Dreamers Project, a non-profit immigration legal services organization. A humanitarian at heart, and creative problem solver by nature, Sylvia uses visual storytelling to increase understanding of our shared humanity and connection to the natural world. Her work has screened at film festivals around the world, US Congress, the Department of Interior, and dozens of universities and civic institutions. She made her first film as a Fulbright scholar, and she is currently a National Geographic Explorer for her upcoming film project, Mermaids Against Plastic. Sylvia holds an MFA in Film and Media Arts from American University, a Certificate in International Development and Social Change from the Middlebury Institute for International Studies, and a BA in International Studies from Middlebury College. She has taught film at the Corcoran College of Art + Design and the Santa Fe University of Art and Design.
