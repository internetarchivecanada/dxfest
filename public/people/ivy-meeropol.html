Biography

Ivy Meeropol is a producer and director of an array of acclaimed documentary feature films and television series. Bully. Coward. Victim: The Story of Roy Cohn is her latest film. In 2016, Meeropol directed and produced the documentary film, Indian Point, which film explores the issues surrounding the use of nuclear energy by looking at the Indian Point Energy Center, located just 35 miles from the heart of New York City. Meeropol also directed a six-part documentary series for the Sundance Channel entitled The Hill. It focused on the work of four young staff members at the office of congressman Robert Wexler (D-FL) as they responded to his electoral defeat in the 2004 presidential election. The documentary aired on the Sundance Channel in August and September 2006. Meeropol is also the director and producer of the 2004 documentary Heir to an Execution, which explored the legacy of her grandparents, Ethel and Julius Rosenberg.

Other bios (1)

Ivy Meeropol directed and produced the HBO documentary BULLY. COWARD. VICTIM. THE STORY OF ROY COHN, which will premiere at the 2019 New York Film Festival. Also two short films for the Emmy Award-winning documentary series YEARS OF LIVING DANGEROUSLY, and INDIAN POINT. She was the senior story producer for the 2017 CNN Films feature-length documentary THE END: THE FINAL DAYS INSIDE THE OBAMA WHITE HOUSE. Other litany of work include DEATH ROW STORIES, THE HILL, and HEIR TO AN EXECUTION, which explored the legacy of her grandparents, Ethel and Julius Rosenberg.
