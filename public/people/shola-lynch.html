Biography

Shola Lynch is award-winning director and producer with twenty years of experience. She has written and directed for CNN, BET, ESPN, HBO Sports, MSNBC, TV One and PBS. Lynch is also the Curator of film and audio at The New York Public Library’s Schomburg Center for Research in Black Culture.

Her independent film work has screened at film festivals, theaters and broadcast around the world and continues to be a staple in classrooms, inspiring scholarship from grade school reports to dissertations. Her work has been recognized with a Peabody, Emmy and an NAACP Image award among others.

Chisholm ‘72 — Unbought & Unbossed, about Brooklyn Congresswoman Shirley Chisholm’s groundbreaking run for president in 1972, premiered at the Sundance Film Festival, was nominated for two Spirit Awards and aired on PBS’ POV series in 2005. Lynch captured the spirit of Mrs. Chisholm’s political fire through what would be her last on-camera interview. The documentary has reshaped Chisholm’s historical legacy and has since become a classic.

Free Angela & All Political Prisoners recounts the politically charged events that thrust academic and activist Angela Davis on to the FBI’s Ten Most Wanted list. After a gala premier at the Toronto International Film Festival, the documentary sold internationally in Brazil, France, South Africa and Sweden. In the US, Code Black/Lionsgate distributed the film theatrically before it broadcast on BET in 2014.

In 2013, Sundance selected Lynch for the prestigious Women’s Filmmaker Initiative. Her current project, The Outlaw, her first narrative feature, has been honored with a highly competitive 2015 Creative Capital grant. Lynch is also a 2016 member of Academy of Motion Picture Arts & Sciences, where she has served on the Documentary Executive Branch Committee and is currently on the Diversity Committee.
