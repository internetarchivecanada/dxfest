Biography

Andalusia Knoll Soloff is a multimedia journalist based in Mexico City. She is a freelance video producer for Al Jazeera, Democracy Now!, VICE News, BBC, ZDF, TeleSUR, and NBC Latino. Her work has also appeared in The Intercept, Teen Vogue, Latino USA, and New York Magazine. Her reporting focuses on human resilience and dignity in the face of disappearances, state violence, land struggles and gender-based murders in Latin America. She is the founder and coordinator of the new journalist organization, Frontline Freelance México, collaborates with the Mexican Periodistas de a Pie network and has been active in the press freedom movement in Mexico.

Knoll Soloff was awarded the Intercept/Double Exposure/ Screening Group Investigative Film Grant, The Adelante Fellowship with the IWMF, The Leonard C. Goodman Fellowship and the PRENDE Judicial Reporting scholarship at the Iberoamerican University. She is the author of the graphic novel Alive You Took Them, about the 43 disappeared Ayotzinapa students. When she’s not reporting, she keeps busy as a DJ, printmaker, and urban cyclist.

Andalusia Knoll Soloff will be a panelist on The Undertow panel and her film, POPPY CRASH screens in Short Cuts Program 1.
