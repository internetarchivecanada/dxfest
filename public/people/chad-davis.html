Biography

Originally from Indianapolis, Chad Davis is currently studying Culture and Politics at Georgetown University in Washington DC. Chad’s passion for film grew out of his love for acting. He has been a part of a world champion improvisational acting team for 10 years, through which he developed a passion for directing and scene work. Chad is president of the Georgetown Independent Film Society at Georgetown, a club on campus dedicated to building a bridge between Washington and Hollywood. He is also extensively involved in programs combating issues related to the environment and immigration.
