Biography

Anthony Edwards is an award-winning actor, producer, and director whose career spans film, television, and independent storytelling. He is best known for his beloved performances as Dr. Mark Greene on ER and as “Goose” in Top Gun. A Golden Globe and Screen Actors Guild Award winner, Edwards has appeared in acclaimed films including Revenge of the Nerds, Miracle Mile, and The Client. Beyond acting, he has produced and directed a range of projects for both screen and television and currently serves as executive producer of Norah Shapiro’s documentary Magic & Monsters. Passionate about authentic storytelling and creative collaboration, Edwards continues to champion projects that connect and inspire audiences worldwide.
