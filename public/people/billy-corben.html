Biography

Emmy, Peabody, and Edward R. Murrow Award-winning filmmaker Billy Corben is a Florida native, lifelong Miamian and co-founder of the Miami Beach-based production company rakontur. He has directed such documentaries as the Sundance Film Festival selection Deal: A Question of Consent (2001), the Cocaine Cowboys franchise, including the Netflix Original Cocaine Cowboys: The Kings of Miami (2021), Dawg Fight (2015), TIFF World Premiere Screwball (2018), the HBO Original 537 Votes (2020), executive produced by Adam McKay (The Big Short, Vice), and the ESPN 30 for 30s The U (2009), Broke (2012) and The U Part 2 (2014). His latest, God Forbid: The Sex Scandal That Brought Down a Dynasty (2022), is the most-watched doc in Hulu history.
