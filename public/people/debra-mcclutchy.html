Biography

Debra McClutchy is an independent filmmaker based in Brooklyn, NY. Her most recent independent film credits include co-producer of The Booksellers, a documentary about the New York rare book world premiering at NYFF 2019. She’s also a senior creative producer at Oscilloscope Laboratories, a film distribution company in New York City. Debra joined Oscilloscope in 2009 and produces home video extra features and digital content such as The House that Yauch Built, an online, multimedia experience about the company. Previously, she was a producer for The Criterion Collection, and an associate producer in documentary television in San Francisco, where she won an Emmy Award for A Matter of Seconds, a program about the 1989 earthquake.

Debra McClutchy will be presenting her film project Martha on the Crossing Borders workshop with her colleague Anne Alvergue.
