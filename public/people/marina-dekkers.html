    Biography

    Marina Dekkers is a Festival Assistant at the Double Exposure Investigative Film Festival and Symposium. Marina grew up in Düsseldorf, Germany, and is now a student at Georgetown University, studying Marketing with a passion for journalism and film. On campus, Marina is the host of a public radio show at Georgetown University’s WGTB radio station and the director of the student-run convenience store Hoya Snaxa, part of the world’s largest student-run non-profit, Students of Georgetown Inc. Marina’s interest in film began at a New York Film Academy program in Boston where she directed her first film short, and since, she’s a fan of the work of David Fincher, Phoebe Waller-Bridge, Alfonso Cuarón, and many more. Marina was first exposed to the DX Festival in a Documentary film class that cemented her love and appreciation of cinema and storytelling in general. As a fan of the festival, she is very excited to be working with the team as a Festival Assistant, mainly in outreach and marketing.
