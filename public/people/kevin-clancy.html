Biography

Kevin Clancy is the co-founder and creative director of Evident Media, a nonprofit newsroom producing video journalism for the public good. In its first year, Evident’s work has reached more than 10 million viewers and earned a national Emmy Award. Before launching Evident, Clancy co-developed and served as senior international producer on the eight-time Emmy-nominated series In Real Life, leading production on over 30 episodes filmed across 25 countries.
