Biography

Kyle O’Connor specializes in Marketing, Events and Corporate Partnerships. She most recently served as Managing Director of the international film festival AFI DOCS.

Kyle worked with The George Washington University School of Media and Public Affairs as Producer of the 2013 “Feeding the Planet Summit” and Managing Director of “Face the Facts USA.”

Before that, Kyle was Vice President of Corporate Affairs and Social Responsibility for Discovery Communications where she oversaw all aspects of Discovery’s corporate partnerships, including the creation of the Discovery Channel Young Scientist Challenge, AFI-Discovery Channel Silverdocs (now AFI DOCS) and DCI’s role as a Partner of America’s National Parks. Kyle’s previous positions over her 19 years at Discovery included director of retail marketing and senior management roles in affiliate, ad sales, and education marketing.

She received a bachelor’s degree in Communications from the University of North Carolina at Chapel Hill. She lives in Silver Spring, MD with her two children.
