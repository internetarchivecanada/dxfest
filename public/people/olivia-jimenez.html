Biography

Olivia Jimenez works on designing graphics for the festival. Originally from Miami, Florida, she is currently studying at the Film & TV School in the Academy of Performing Arts in Prague (FAMU) to get her Master’s degree in film editing. Previously, she attended Georgetown University with double majors in English Literature & Psychology, and a minor in Film & Media Studies. While her primary work is in editing, she enjoys the similar creative challenges that come with design and is excited to be a part of Double Exposure.

From the festival website (archived), 2023

Other bios (1)

Olivia Jimenez is a festival assistant and graphic designer at the Double Exposure Investigative Film Festival and Symposium. She is from Miami, FL and recently graduated Georgetown University with majors in English and Psychology and a minor in Film & Media Studies. On campus, she co-led and designed for the literary magazine, Bossier. She also designed promotional materials and assisted in the planning of OWN IT Summit and hoyaTHON, Georgetown’s Dance Marathon benefiting Children’s National.

She has fallen in love with film and storytelling, which is why she will be continuing her studies of film editing and directing at the Film and TV School of the Academy of Performing Arts in Prague (FAMU). In the past, she has worked as a production assistant for an independent feature film, Marcus, shot in Miami. Olivia is excited to be a part of the Double Exposure team, and can’t wait to see all that the festival has in store.

From the festival website (archived), 2021
