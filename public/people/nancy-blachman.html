    Biography

    Nancy Blachman has collaborated with filmmakers and film teams since 2015 to bring powerful stories—highlighting pressing issues and extraordinary individuals—to a wider audience, inspiring viewers to take action. Among others, Blachman has supported critically acclaimed films including Crip Camp, Aftershock, Athlete A, and The Social Dilemma. Since 2022, she has served as a judge for the Documentary Emmys.

    Blachman often watches documentary films at home while rowing on her indoor rower.
