Biography

Sam Benstead is a filmmaker acclaimed for bringing warmth, big picture visuals and cinematic flair to the most difficult subjects. His work includes the hit Netflix series The Puppetmaster, which led to the arrest of conman Robert Freegard after its release; and award-winning Mumbai High, the first Bollywood musical documentary ever made. He was Director of Photography on Bobi Wine: The People’s President, which premiered at the Venice Film Festival before being nominated for an Oscar.
