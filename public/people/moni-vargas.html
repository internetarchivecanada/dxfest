    Biography

    Moni Vargas is an Emmy-nominated filmmaker and multidisciplinary artist whose art and practice spans music, theater and photography. She is the Creative and Program Director at Breakthrough US, a nonprofit culture change organization that uses media to promote human rights through a feminist lens. Moni has directed an array of music videos, editorials, short documentaries, commercial and educational content, including Queen Latifah’s 11-week Confidence course for Strayer University. In 2012 & 2017, Moni produced and directed the RED TABLE TALK pilots for Jada Pinkett-Smith. Moni is the co-creator of FRIENDS WE LOVE, a multimedia platform featuring a short documentary series, media workshops and experiential events. Her film work has screened at international film festivals and at New York’s prestigious Brooklyn Museum and The Bronx Museum. In addition to leading Breakthrough’s creative team, Moni is the cofounder of Nuyorktricity Productions in Los Angeles. An Afro-Mestiza Latina, she is passionate about immigrant and women’s rights, inclusive practices, broad representation and uplifting the creative projects of youth. She is a member of the Alliance of Women Directors, BGDM, NALIP and ACLU.

    Moni will be moderating the Trauma-Informed Storytelling as a Portal to Heal workshop.
