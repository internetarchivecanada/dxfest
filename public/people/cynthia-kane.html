Biography

U.S. Producer Cynthia Kane formed GBGG Productions with Lisa Scott Gordon and John Warburton (UK producers) with the mission to produce social issue fiction and documentary film.

In creating DOCday on Sundance Channel in 2002, she helped transform the way global documentaries are seen on American television. Through her work at ITVS, she shepherded over 150 domestic and international co-productions for public media.

As a commissioning editor for documentaries, she recently helped launch Al Jazeera America and the Sunday night premiere doc strand, Al Jazeera America Presents.

From the festival website (archived), 2021

Other bios (1)

Cynthia Kane works as senior commissioning producer in Al Jazeera America’s Doc Unit. Previously, she was at ITVS (Independent Television Service) managing the international initiative for funding, and also supported the domestic funding initiatives. She worked at Sundance Channel and co-created DOCday during her 10-year tenure there.
