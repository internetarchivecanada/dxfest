    Biography

    Lois Lipman (Producer/Director, Downwind) has developed and field produced documentaries for CBS’ 60 Minutes, the BBC and Channel 4 – UK, winning an Emmy and Best Documentary from American Women in Radio and TV. Her work has taken her throughout Europe, India, Cuba, South Africa, Israel, and the United States. She holds an MFA in Film and Video from American University where her thesis film A Place to Belong, about boys bullied for having Asperger’s Syndrome, won a CINE Golden Eagle and was broadcast on PBS. She is currently at work on her first independent documentary feature, Downwind, about the victims of the world’s first nuclear bomb – the Trinity Bomb in New Mexico – and their journey for recognition, compensation and justice.
