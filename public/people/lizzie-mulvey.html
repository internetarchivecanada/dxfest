Biography

Lizzie Mulvey is a documentary filmmaker and investigative journalist based in Brooklyn. She has worked for PBS FRONTLINE and A+E as an associate producer, and has published stories in The Guardian, Al Jazeera, USA Today and more. She grew up in New Jersey and holds a dual master’s degree in journalism and data science from Columbia University.
