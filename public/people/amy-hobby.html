Biography

Amy Hobby is a co-founder of Distribution Advocates,  a coalition to advance access and transparency in the structures of distribution. Hobby oversees the Film-Ade Fund for Distribution Advocates, which provides grants for innovation in audience building.

Hobby previously served as the Executive Director of the nonprofit Tribeca Film Institute from 2016-2020. She is also an Academy Award-nominated and Emmy Award-winning producer whose films have premiered at Sundance, Berlin, Cannes, SXSW, Tribeca, Telluride (among other festivals).

Amy Hobby co-founded Distribution Advocates, advancing access and transparency in distribution. She oversees the Film-Ade Fund and previously led the Tribeca Film Institute. An Academy Award-nominated and Emmy Award-winning producer, her films have premiered at major festivals worldwide.

From the 2024 catalogue, p. 44

Amy Hobby has 20 years experience as a film producer. Her work spans feature-length dramas, documentaries, international features, television pilots and webisodes. Hobby mentors emerging filmmakers and supports women directors through initiatives launched through Tangerine Entertainment, which she co-founded. Her producing projects include SECRETARY, HAMLET (with Ethan Hawke), LOVE, MARILYN, and WHAT HAPPENED, MISS SIMONE?, for which she was nominated for an Academy Award and won an Emmy and a Peabody.
