Biography

Maxyne Franklin is one of the founding directors of Doc Society, a nonprofit foundation with teams based in Europe, East Africa, the Americas, and Australia, dedicated to supporting independent filmmaking globally. Since 2005, we have supported 450+ projects and directly regranted $22M to filmmakers in over 40 countries, leveraging our support to unlock tens of millions more in resources for the field.

In addition to grantmaking for artists and journalists and supporting their creative process, Doc Society helps creatives develop impact strategies and connect with communities and allies beyond the media industry and across civil society. Maxyne is a proud Executive Producer of over 40 films, including 4 Academy Award nominees and 4 Peabody winners. Recent highlights have included Petra Costa’s The Edge of Democracy, Vinay Shukla’s While We Watched, Andrea Arnold’s Cow, and David Osit’s Mayor.

Maxyne Franklin is one of the founding directors of BRITDOC and shares responsibility for overseeing the film funding programs. Franklin has been the executive producer of a number of award-winning films including VIRUNGA, ALL THESE SLEEPLESS NIGHTS, HOOLIGAN SPARROW, THE POSSIBILITIES ARE ENDLESS, THE SQUARE, PUSSY RIOT, HELL & BACK AGAIN, PING PONG AND AFGHAN STAR. She now heads up the US office of BRITDOC.
