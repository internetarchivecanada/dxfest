Biography

Yvonne Ng is an audiovisual archivist and Senior Manager of Training and Partnerships at WITNESS, a global organization that helps people use video to document the truth and defend human rights. She serves on the steering committee of the Trust in Archives Initiative (TAI), a coalition dedicated to preserving the authenticity of media collections, encouraging transparency, and ensuring public trust in archives. Yvonne holds an M.A in Moving Image Archiving and Preservation from New York University, and was the 2024 recipient of the Alan Stark Award from the Association of Moving Image Archivists in recognition of her contributions to moving image archives and the association.
