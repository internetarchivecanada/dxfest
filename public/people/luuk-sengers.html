    Biography

    Luuk Sengers is an environmental journalist and teacher. He currently writes for the leading Dutch magazine De Groene Amsterdammer. In the past, he has worked as an economy reporter at NRC Handelsblad, Associated Press Services, Quote, and Intermediair. Luuk has also trained thousands of professionals, on five continents, in investigative methods, interview techniques, and data journalism. Together with Mark Lee Hunter, he developed the Story-Based Inquiry method for investigative journalists, which is widely acclaimed as a benchmark for education in the profession.

    He is (co-)author of several manuals for investigative journalism, of which Story-Based Inquiry: A Manual for Investigative Journalists (2009) has been adopted by UNESCO, and published in fifteen languages. His most recent book with Mark Lee Hunter, Modern Investigative Journalism: A Comprehensive Curriculum, was published by Arabic Reporters for Investigative Journalism in 2019.

    Luuk teaches in newsrooms and at universities at home and abroad, including the Thomas More University of Applied Sciences in Mechelen (Belgium) and the Vrije Universiteit in Brussels. He advised NGOs, including Greenpeace and Friends of the Earth, and regularly speaks at IRE, GIJC, CIJ, and Data Harvest conferences, the world’s leading forums for best practices in investigative journalism.

    For ten years he was on the board of the VVOJ, the Dutch-Flemish association of investigative journalists. Luuk studied Social-Economic History at Radboud University Nijmegen. When he is not working, he hikes in nature with a good camera in his backpack.

    Luuk will be facilitating the Story-Based Inquiry I and Story-Based Inquiry II workshops.
