Biography

Film editorial/business consultant, producer & sales agent, Laetitia Doyle has worked in Paris, San Francisco, and relocated to Washington DC, where she created Giansily-Doyle Media Consulting LLC. (GDMC) in 2012.

Her strengths include identifying diverse talent in the early stages of project development, providing editorial oversight, and championing initiatives from selection through contract management.

She is representing Paris-based distribution company Terranoa since 2011, and is responsible for sales, copros and for sourcing programming on her territories for Terranoa’s catalogue.

Laetitia is also the President of the non-profit organization French In Motion whose mission is to foster transatlantic collaboration, in particular through the US & French Connection Program, run in partnership with the New-York based Gotham Film & Media Institute since 2016.

Ever present in the industry, Laetitia frequently speaks on or moderates panels at conferences and serves on awards juries, including the International Emmy Awards, IDA Documentary Awards, and the Jackson Hole Wildlife Film Festival.

In 2015, she was the recipient of a TIVA Award for ‘A Salute to Excellence in Media Distribution.
