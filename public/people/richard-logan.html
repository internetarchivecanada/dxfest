Biography

Richard Logan, known for his charm and smiling disposition, is the President of The Reva and David Logan Foundation (RDLF). The foundation is a major funder of investigative journalism in all media formats, and is actively involved in supporting a range of documentary film productions.

“We are always looking for film-makers with vision and courage, the ability to ask timely questions, and the experience and skills to bring their projects to completion.”

Richard is a believer in deep due diligence, innovative grant making, betting on the jockey (not the horse) as well as the curative powers of curry.

Richard E. Logan is president of The Reva and David Logan Foundation, and founding executive of a UK-based Mac software company, Richard has been a hands-on funder/participant in projects across a wide range of disciplines worldwide, including independent media of all kinds – radio, film, print–and more. He takes a hands-on approach to the causes and organizations he supports, increasing the efficacy of grant recipients through constant evaluation to discover new synergies.
