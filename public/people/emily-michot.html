Biography

Emily Michot has been a staff photographer and videographer at the Miami Herald for the past 25 years. Michot has worked as the visual journalist with the Miami Herald’s I-Team on some of their most acclaimed projects over the past five years, including Innocents Lost. License to Launder, Beyond Punishment, and Fight Club. Both License to Launder and Fight Club were finalists for the Pulitzer Prize.

This past year, Michot spent a year working with Miami Herald investigative reporter Julie K. Brown, interviewing young women who were teenagers when they were sexually abused by multimillionaire Jeffrey Epstein. The series, Perversion of Justice, examined how Alexander Acosta, a future Trump Cabinet member gave Epstein, a suspected sex trafficker, a secret plea deal that allowed him to escape a life sentence in federal prison. Epstein was arrested eight months after the series ran, and Acosta resigned his position as Labor Secretary. Epstein was later found dead in his jail cell from what has been ruled a suicide.

Emily Michot will be a panelist on the #MeToo 2.0 panel.
