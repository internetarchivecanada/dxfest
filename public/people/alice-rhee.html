    Biography

    Alice Rhee is a two-time Emmy award winning television producer and multimedia journalist. At present, she is the Editorial Director for Folo Media, a nonprofit journalism organization that reports on inequality and inequity in San Antonio. She is also a program advisor for the Tribeca Film Institute working with the If/Then Short Documentary initiative. From 2013-2015, she was the Senior Producer for Digital Video at The Washington Post. Prior to this, she spent the bulk of her career as a breaking news and special events producer at NBC News hq in New York City.
