Biography

Nichole Christian is a writer, editor, and native Detroiter. She is currently the managing editor for Outlier Media, based in Detroit. Nichole has written extensively about the role artists play in cities’ cultural revitalization. She is the co-author of the award-winning public art book, Canvas Detroit. Previously, she worked as a staff writer for The New York Times, The Wall Street Journal, Time magazine, and the Detroit Free Press. Her background also includes stints curating and moderating community engagement conversations for the former Freep Film Festival and arts nonprofits in the Midwest. Nichole is also a proud mom: One daughter, one dog.
