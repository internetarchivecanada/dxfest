Biography

Shayna Wilson is the proud mom of 3 beautiful girls. Shayna began learning American sign language while taking parent sign language classes at the school for the deaf when her daughter was just 3 years old in 2004. Shayna further immersed herself in deaf culture by becoming a residential counselor at The American School for the deaf from 2011-2017. Shayna has gone on to advocate for her daughter throughout the state of Connecticut on issues such as childcare reform with a focus on children with mental health needs.

Shayna currently serves in a dual role as an Independent Living Advocate for the deaf & hard of hearing at The Independence Unlimited Center, and as a Vocational Advocate for the deaf and hard of hearing for the Disabilities Network of Eastern Connecticut.

Shayna is the founder of a community-based organization called NexIzNow. NextIzNow is an organization that promotes advocacy through a holistic perspective in the greater Hartford area and abroad. The goal is to provide all caregivers the resources they need to advocate for adequate healthcare, educational, and wrap-around services through a communication matrix that encourages parents to become a proprietary component in their child’s treatment and care. Shayna remains passionate about advocacy for all families with children with disabilities.

Shayna will be a panelist on the Age of Consent panel, and in association with WAITING FOR TEARAH screening in Short Cuts Program 1
