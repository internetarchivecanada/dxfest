Biography

Anne Alvergue is an award-winning documentary filmmaker and editor. Her short films have screened internationally at film festivals and galleries, including the Pacific Film Archive, Galapagos Gallery, San Francisco Art Institute, Arsenal Berlin, Black Maria, Mill Valley, and Centro De La Imagen. Her editing credits include Love, Gilda; Bully. Coward. Victim: The Story of Roy Cohn; That Way Madness Lies, Museum Town, Being Elmo, Life After Hoop Dreams, Tibet in Song, My Kid Could Paint That, and Married in America.  Anne received a Bachelor’s Degree from UC Berkeley and a Master’s Degree in Documentary Film from Stanford University.

Anne Alvergue will be presenting her film project Martha on the Crossing Borders workshop with her colleague Debra McClutchy.
