Biography

Lea Trusty is a Program Associate on the Public Square team at Democracy Fund, a private foundation working to ensure that our political system is able to withstand new challenges and deliver on its promise to the American people.

Lea supports the Engaged Journalism strategy in its mission to invest in innovations and institutions that focus on creating audience-centered, inclusive, and trusted newsrooms that reflect the diversity of their communities. She leads their portfolio centered on diversity, equity, and inclusion in journalism. Current grantees include organizations like the Maynard Institute, Ida B. Wells Society, and Take the Lead.

Lea joined Democracy Fund in summer 2017. Prior to joining the team, Lea was a Newman’s Own Foundation Fellow, a program for recent graduates interested in philanthropy and social impact. She was placed at WSHU Public Radio, an NPR affiliate in Connecticut. She covered a wide breadth of stories, from minority teacher recruitment to the first family suing ICE for detention center abuses.

Lea is currently a member of the EPIP DC steering committee. She graduated magna cum laude from Princeton University, where she studied politics and Latin America.

Lea will be moderating the In the Crosshairs: Doxxing, Trolling and the Weaponization of Social Media panel.
