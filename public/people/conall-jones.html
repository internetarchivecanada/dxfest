Biography

Conall Jones is an Oscar-nominated producer whose work spans feature documentaries, short films, series, and investigative video journalism. His films have screened at major festivals such as Telluride, Toronto, Tribeca, and the Berlinale, and his work has appeared on platforms including Netflix, Vice, CNN, A&E, and The New Yorker. Conall spent three years as a staff video journalist in The Wall Street Journal newsroom. He was nominated for an Academy Award in 2023 for producing the short documentary Stranger at the Gate, executive produced by Malala Yousafzai.
