Biography

Merete Mueller is a documentary director based in New York. Her work explores our relationships to built and natural environments, and has included in-depth reporting on climate and infrastructure, as well as intimate stories about body image, homes, and belonging. Merete’s films have screened at SXSW, Hot Docs, DOC NYC and The Brooklyn Museum, via Al Jazeera, the Canadian Broadcasting Channel, Netflix, The New York Times Op-Docs series,

and The New Yorker.
