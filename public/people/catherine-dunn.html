Biography

Catherine Dunn is a reporter with the investigative team at Fusion, where she collaborated on the global exposé of offshore secrets in the Panama Papers. Previously, she covered consumer protection issues as a senior writer at International Business Times. She has also written for Fortune, People magazine, and Newsweek. Dunn was a finalist for a Livingston award and a Deadline Club award for her reporting on the dangers of defective guns. Her writing on the U.S. affordable housing crisis and vulnerable families was recognized by the Society for Features Journalism.

Other bios (1)

Catherine Dunn is an investigative reporter with Fusion at Univision, where she’s been digging into the Panama Papers trove of leaked documents on the offshore financial world. Previously, she was a senior writer at the International Business Times, where she covered consumer finance and legal issues. She wrote about racial profiling in retail, the shortage of affordable housing in New Orleans, and the unregulated hazards of defective guns.
