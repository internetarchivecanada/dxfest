Biography

Jennifer Huang is committed to creating insight into misunderstood

issues and advancing social justice. She has been working in documentary and television

production for 17 years. She co-produced From Baghdad to the Bay, following an Iraqi

refugee’s life as an out gay man; and Standing on Sacred Ground, a global series about

indigenous people protecting their sacred sites. She was also associate producer for

Jacques Pépin: The Art of Craft, an American Masters broadcast. At JAK Doc (a

department of Lucasfilm) she wrote and produced Harlem’s Hellfighters: Black Soldiers

of World War I, and associate-produced nine other short films. Additionally, she has

worked as a writer, field producer and associate producer on programs for PBS,

Anonymous Content, the Travel Channel, HGTV, and TNT. In 2010, she helped develop

an augmented reality concept at BAVC’s Producers Institute, and in 2013 was a BAVC

MediaMaker fellow. She is also an inaugural member of Re-Present Media’s fellowship

program. Jennifer graduated from UC Berkeley with a BA in Social Welfare and

Ethnography through Cinema.

Jennifer will be presenting her project, The Long Rescue, at the Docs in Progress Peer Pitch.
