Biography

Michael I Schiller is the Creative Director of The Center for Investigative Reporting and an Emmy Award ® winning producer and director. Michael uses animation, radio, video, photography and design to tell stories. His work has garnered some of journalism’s most prestigious prizes including a national Edward R. Murrow Award and a National Society of Professional Journalists Sigma Delta Chi Award. Michael’s animated short film THE BOX, about solitary confinement of youth, won a San Francisco Film Festival Golden Gate Award and a New Orleans Film Festival’s Special Jury Prize.

From the festival website (archived), 2021
