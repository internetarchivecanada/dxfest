Biography

Born and raised in Pakistan, Sobia Ahmad moved to the U.S. at the age of fourteen. Highlighting the inseparability of the self and larger power structures, her interdisciplinary work dissects how political, historical, and cultural forces shape intimate personal narratives and community experiences. Ahmad’s work has been reviewed in Al-Jazeera English and the Washington Post and has been exhibited internationally—including at the Johnson Museum of Art in New York, and Queen Mary University in London. She received the 2019 Next Generation/Sanctuary Fellowship at VisArts in Maryland, and is currently a fellow at Halcyon Arts Lab in Washington DC. www.sobiaahmad.com.

From the festival website (archived), 2021

Other bios (1)

Sobia Ahmad was born and raised in Pakistan, and moved to the United States at the age of fourteen. Highlighting the inseparability of the self and larger power structures, her interdisciplinary work dissects how spiritual, social, and historical, shape intimate personal narratives and broader community experiences. A strong believer in the power of art for social justice, Ahmad explores how our deeply intimate struggles of belonging can inform larger conversations about national identity, notion of home, cultural memory, and gender.
