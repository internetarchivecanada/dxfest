Biography

George Kevin Jordan is a writer, author, illustrator, and visual artist whose work explores community, place, identity, and the outdoors. He serves as a Community Connector with The 51st, engaging Ward 7 residents in shaping local journalism, and as Lead Trainer for HumanitiesDC’s Community Journalism Program. His creative work spans journalism, fiction, essays, illustration, and visual art.
