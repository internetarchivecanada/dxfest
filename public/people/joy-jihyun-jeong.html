Biography

Joy (she/her) is a video producer with experience across digital/social video, long-form documentaries and broadcast news. Currently she works with Eastern Standard Times, a new platform dedicated to stories from Asia, where she produces micro docs, explainers, news reports, true crime series, and more. Before EST, Joy worked as a story producer with director Dawn Porter and Trilogy Films on an upcoming feature documentary. At Great Big Story, she produced short docs about food, travel, culture and science reaching 12 million+ viewers across YouTube and other social platforms.

At CNN International she covered major breaking news as a producer and video editor. Her thesis film at Columbia Journalism School about black dancers in classical ballet won a Student Academy Award. Currently based in Brooklyn, New York, Joy has two other homes: Seoul, South Korea where she lived until 12 years old, and the good ol’ suburbs of New Jersey where she first learned the nuances of American culture. Joy believes that storytelling is one of the most effective tools in building understanding and connection beyond cultural differences.
