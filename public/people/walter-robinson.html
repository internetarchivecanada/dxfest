Biography

Walter V. Robinson, who led the Globe team that investigated clergy sexual abuse, has been the Globe’s Editor At Large since 2014, when he returned to the paper after seven years as Distinguished Professor of Journalism at Northeastern University. During more than four decades at the Globe, he did local investigative reporting and was chief of Globe City Hall and State House bureaus. He covered four presidential elections, and spent seven years in Washington for the Globe, where he covered the White House during the Reagan and (first) Bush Presidencies. He was the Globe’s Middle East bureau chief during the first Persian Gulf War. He was the Globe city editor and metro editor before becoming Spotlight editor in 2000.In addition to sharing in the Pulitzer for Public Service for the clergy abuse scandal, he and his team were Pulitzer finalists in 2007 for a series on debt collection abuses. Robinson has received honorary degrees from Emerson College and Northeastern University. He is the co-author of “Betrayal – Crisis in the Catholic Church.”

Other bios (1)

Walter Robinson, editor at large at the Boston Globe, has been a reporter and editor there for 43 years. He covered city, state and national politics and government, and was the newspaper’s Middle East correspondent during the First Gulf War. He led the investigation that exposed the Catholic Church’s cover-up of decades of sexual abuse by nearly 200 priests.
