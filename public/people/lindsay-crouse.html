Biography

Lindsay Crouse is a producer for the New York Times Op-Docs, a collection of more than 300 Oscar-nominated and Emmy-winny short documentaries by independent filmmakers.

Lindsay Crouse will be a panelist on The Visual Newsroom panel.

From the festival website (archived), 2021

Other bios (1)

Lindsay Crouse is the coordinating producer for The New York Times Op-Docs series. Since joining Op-Docs in 2011, she has worked on nearly 300 short films, VR, and interactive documentaries that won two Emmy Awards, two Peabodys, and two Oscar nominations for best short documentary.
