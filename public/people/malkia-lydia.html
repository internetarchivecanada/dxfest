Biography

Malkia K. Lydia has produced short films installed at the Smithsonian Institution, National Civil Rights Museum, and other historical centers. Currently, she is directing two long-form documentaries that combine participatory, verité and archival methods. Malkia is a seasoned archival producer, having supported investigative, true crime, biographical, sports, and music documentaries in that capacity.
