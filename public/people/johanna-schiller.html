Biography

[vc_row][vc_column][vc_column_text]Johanna Schiller is a producer based in Brooklyn, NY. For over a decade she produced special edition video releases of classic films for the Criterion Collection. Currently she works primarily in non-fiction television and documentary film, producing documentary features and working on series for History Channel, CNN and more. Johanna co-founded the Brooklyn Film Festival Exchange, an event that provides filmmakers with access to industry experts and panel discussions on relevant topics, and she curates the Exchange’s annual highlight, a documentary film pitch session.[/vc_column_text][/vc_column][/vc_row][vc_row][vc_column][vc_empty_space height=”12px”][/vc_column][/vc_row]
