Biography

Rachel Grady is the co-owner of New York’s Loki Films, Grady is the co-director of Jesus Camp (Oscar nominee), The Boys of Baraka (Emmy nominee), 12th & Delaware (Peabody winner), DETROPIA (Sundance and Emmy winner) and Freakonomics: The Movie. Last year Grady co-directed Norman Lear: Just Another Version of You, the opening night selection at the 2016 Sundance Film Festival. The film was recently nominated for an Emmy alongside PBS’ American Masters. She and co-director Heidi Ewing spent three years on One of Us, which is their sixth feature-length documentary collaboration together. Rachel is a member of the Directors Guild and the Academy of Motion Picture Arts and Sciences. She resides in Brooklyn, N.Y.
