Biography

Heidi Reinberg is currently producing the UNTITLED CRIMINAL JUSTICE / HS PROJECT alongside that film’s director, Emmy winner Maisie Crow. The film is a collaboration with Anonymous Content and The New York Times, and is repped by Cinetic Media. Additionally, she served as Consulting Producer on director Tania Cypriano’s Born to Be, which will premiere at this fall’s New York Film Festival. Reinberg produced the ITVS co-production 93Queen, which won the inaugural Hot Docs Forum first look Pitch Prize before premiering at the Hot Docs Documentary Festival. The film was distributed theatrically via Abramorama, and was held over for 6 weeks at NYC IFC Film Center before airing on POV and arte SWR. To date, 93Queen has played at more than 75 film festivals around the world. Previously, Reinberg worked with Oscar winners Ross Kauffman and Cynthia Wade, and award-winning directors Mai Iskander and Jeremiah Zagar. Her work has been supported by the Sundance Documentary Institute with support of the Open Society Foundation and Just Films | Ford Foundation; the Winkelried Foundation; XTR; the Tribeca Film Institute with support from the Oath Foundation; Catapult Film Fund; Fork Films; NYSCA; the IDA Enterprise Fund; the IDA Pare Lorentz Doc Fund; the Economic Hardship Reporting Project; Picture Motion; the Hartley Film Foundation; the Austin Film Society; the TFI A&E StoryLab; Women Make Movies; and the Independent Filmmaker Project.

Heidi Reinberg will be a facilitator on the Grant Writing Workshop.
