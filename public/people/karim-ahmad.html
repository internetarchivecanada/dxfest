Biography

Karim Ahmad is a writer, cultural strategist, husband and father, member of the Guild of Future Architects, and Director of the Outreach & Inclusion Program at the Sundance Institute. At Sundance Karim is working to build a just and beautiful future for artists from historically marginalized communities by designing and overseeing emerging and mid-career artist support programs. He is the main author of the Restoring the Future report, a manifestation of a worldbuilding and advocacy initiative co-created by the ALLIED coalition of arts organizations. He was previously a Senior Strategist and Senior Producer at ITVS, and Creator and Showrunner of the groundbreaking sci-fi series, FutureStates. He can be found on Twitter as @thatkarimahmad.
