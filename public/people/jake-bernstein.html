Biography

Over a three-decade career, Jake Bernstein has covered politics, war, high finance and the offshore industry. His work has earned numerous awards and honors. In 2011, while an investigative reporter with the nonprofit newsroom ProPublica, Bernstein and a colleague won the Pulitzer Prize for National Reporting for coverage of Wall Street in the lead up to the financial crisis. More recently, he worked with ICIJ.org on the 2016 project that became known as the Panama Papers. He is currently writing a book about the offshore industry and efforts by journalists and others to reveal its inner workings.

From the festival website (archived), 2021
