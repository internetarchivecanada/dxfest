Biography

Lauren (she/her) is a video journalist and digital strategist whose work has been featured on-air and online at PBS, The New York Times, The Atlantic and The Intercept, among others, and screened in film festivals and events around the world. Currently, Lauren leads the video team at The Intercept.

Director of Video, The Intercept

From the 2022 catalogue, p. 41

Lauren Feeney leads the video team at The Intercept, an online outlet focusing on investigative and adversarial journalism. In addition to The Intercept, her work has been featured on air and online at The New York Times, PBS and Al Jazeera English among other outlets, and in film festivals and events around the world. She divides her time between New York and Boston.

From the 2019 catalogue, p. 52

Lauren Feeney is the director of video production for The Intercept, an investigative news outlet that covers national security, politics, civil liberties, the environment, international affairs, technology, criminal justice, the media, and more. Feeney spent a decade in public media, working on programs including Moyers & Company, Wide Angle and Women, War & Peace. Her work has appeared on-air and online on PBS, The New York Times and The Atlantic among other outlets.
