Biography

Nicki Mayo is the multimedia journalist behind Nicki Mayo News, LLC, an audio/video company specializing in video, podcast and social media campaign production for small businesses, civic groups and faith-based organizations.

The Baltimore native and Syracuse University alumna is the Editor-at-large for Black Women Unmuted. You may have also seen and heard Nicki’s multimedia work recently on TV One’s “News One Now,” “Fatal Attraction,” “Justice By Any Means” and “Thou Shalt Not.”

She recently served three years as the social media director for The Crisis Magazine, the official publication for the National Association for the Advancement of Colored People (NAACP). She was also digital editor for The Philadelphia Tribune‘s PhillyTrib.com website and various social media accounts.
