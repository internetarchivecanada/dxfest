Biography

Andreas Dalsgaard is an independent director and producer and founder of Danish doc production house Elk Film. His most recent directing and writing credits include Venice Days winner The War Show, which took home the Venice Days Award, the hit documentary The Lost Leonardo, the AFI winner Afghan Muscles and Al Jazeera Grand Prix winner The Human Scale. His most recent producing credits include Hacking Hate, As the Tide Comes In and Daughter of Genghis.
