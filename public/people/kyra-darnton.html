Biography

Kyra Darnton is the executive producer of Retro Report. She came to Retro Report from 60 Minutes, where she produced stories covering everything from the Mexican drug war to counterfeit prescription drugs to the cover up in the death of U.S. Ranger Pat Tillman. At 60 Minutes, her investigative reporting and storytelling won multiple awards, including a 2012 Peabody Award for a piece exposing fraud in cancer clinical trials. At Retro Report, she has overseen more than 150 short films with multiple distribution partners ranging from The New York Times to The New Yorker to PBS. Retro Report has won numerous awards including Emmy awards, Edward R. Murrow awards, Webby awards, and a 2018 Gerald Loeb award. Darnton has worked in journalism for 25 years.
