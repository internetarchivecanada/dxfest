Biography

[vc_row][vc_column][vc_column_text]Robin Heller works with nonprofit organizations in Europe, the United States, and the UK to increase their capacity to raise funds. She has worked for such organizations as the Center for Public Integrity, Children’s Defense Fund, Big Brothers Big Sisters of America, and Rare. She is passionate about the way in which journalism and media can change the world for the better. She divides her time between Washington, D.C. and London.
