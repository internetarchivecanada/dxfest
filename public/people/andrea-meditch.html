Biography

Andrea Meditch is a creative producer who founded Back Allie Entertainment to advise and mentor independent filmmakers and festivals. Her films have won Oscars and Emmys; premiered at major international festivals, including Sundance and Toronto; and enjoyed successful theatrical and broadcast runs. She won an Emmy in 2015 for Dangerous Acts; executive-produced Oscar-winner Man on Wire; Oscar-nominated Encounters at the End of the World; and Oscar short-listed Buck. She also executive produced or produced Grizzly Man, In the Shadow of the Moon, The Killer Within, Doubletime and The Flight That Fought Back, among other films. Ernie & Joe premiered in 2019 and has been nominated for two Emmys. She regularly conducts workshops and master classes on story development, creative producing, pitching, and the basics of international film financing and release strategies. Before starting her own company, Andrea held executive and creative positions at Discovery, where she launched the first Discovery Films. She serves as a film expert for the U.S. State Department American Film Showcase and is an advisor to emerging filmmakers and producers at the Sundance Producer Labs, the Hot Docs Forum and Points North non-fiction filmmaker retreats. She has juried numerous festivals and holds a mayoral appointment to the Santa Fe Film and Digital Media Commission, which supports storytellers in northern New Mexico. She holds a PhD in Anthropology with a concentration in media from the University of Texas, and an undergraduate degree in Communications from Northwestern University.

Andrea will be taking meetings for DX Access.
