Biography

Annie Wong is a documentary filmmaker and co-founder of 2OVER10 Media in New York City. She has an extensive background in investigative journalism, most notably through her work on PBS FRONTLINE. Her films explore complex global issues, including war crimes in Ukraine, the rise of violence from online extremism, and cases of medical fraud. Her work has been widely recognized with numerous prestigious awards, including multiple Emmys, the Peabody Award, the Polk Award, the DuPont Award, the Robert F. Kennedy Humanitarian Award, the Overseas Press Club Award, and several Writers Guild of America Awards.
