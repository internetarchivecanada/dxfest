    Biography

    Scott Michels is an award-winning journalist and producer for the Retro Report news and documentary series. His work has appeared in The New York Times, The New Yorker, PBS and ABC News, among other national news outlets. His last collaboration with The New Yorker was nominated for an Emmy Award.
