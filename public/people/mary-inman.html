Biography

Mary Inman is a partner in the San Francisco office of Whistleblower Partners LLP and a veteran whistleblower lawyer, having worked exclusively representing whistleblowers for the past 26 years under the increasing array of U.S. and international whistleblower reward programs. Mary has helped her clients, including Theranos whistleblower Tyler Shultz and Facebook whistleblower Frances Haugen, forge partnerships with the various government agencies who leverage whistleblower information to expose corporate frauds across the tech, defense, healthcare, and finance industries. Mary is an internationally recognized expert on the extraterritorial reach of the U.S. whistleblower reward programs and a regular commentator on all things whistleblower for the New York Times, Wall Street Journal, Bloomberg, BBC, FT, Fortune and Time. Mary was interviewed for a CNBC segment entitled “How corporate whistleblowers make millions” and the recent documentary film about women whistleblowers “When We Speak.”

Head of International Whistleblower Practice, Constantine Cannon LLP

From the 2022 catalogue, p. 44

Mary Inman is a partner in Constantine Cannon’s London Office. She specializes in representing whistleblowers from the U.K., Europe and worldwide under the various American whistleblower reward programs, including the federal and various state False Claims Acts and the SEC, CFTC, IRS and DOT whistleblower programs. Inman's successful representation of three whistleblowers exposing fraud in the Medicare Advantage program was featured in a recent New Yorker magazine article. She is a recognized expert and frequent author and speaker on areas related to the application of the American whistleblower laws internationally and the use of whistleblower laws worldwide.
