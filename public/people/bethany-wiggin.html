Biography

Bethany Wiggin is the Founding Director of the Penn Program in Environmental Humanities at the University of Pennsylvania. Her scholarship explores histories of migration, ecology, language and cultural translation since the Columbian exchange on both sides of the Atlantic world. She experiments with new models of public scholarship and research methods to engage audiences beyond the academy at a time of rapid ecological change. Her work envisions and creates open knowledge communities that connect the arts and sciences and features collaborations with artists and scientists to do so. Projects have been featured on PBS Newshour, CBS Evening News, CNN, The Daily Show with Trevor Noah, Vice, The Washington Post, The New York Times, Science Friday, Die Tagesschau, and Le Monde. Ongoing collaborative, public projects include Data Refuge, An Ecotopian Toolkit, and Rising Waters. She has been a Whiting Public Engagement Fellow and was honored by the Union of Concerned Scientists with a Science Defender award.
