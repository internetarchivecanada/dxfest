    Biography

    Sydney James Harcourt, lead vocalist in Rhapsody on the Outlaw Ocean, is a stage and screen actor from Detroit, Michigan. He’s known for his work on and off Broadway, starring in productions like the cultural phenomenon Hamilton (original Broadway company and 1st replacement for Aaron Burr), Disney’s The Lion King (Simba), and Green Day’s American Idiot. In 2019 he was nominated for Drama Desk and Lortel Awards for originating the role of Joe Scott in the original American cast of Bob Dylan’s Girl From The North Country. He’s since originated the role of Rum Tum Tugger in the critically-acclaimed Cats: The Jellicle Ball Off and On Broadway.

    You’ve seen him in films and TV series, from standard fare (Gone, NCIS: New Orleans, Elementary) to decidedly less standard (a self-harming boxer in musical film I’m Not Gay, the undead demon Edouard who provides eerie countertenor / soprano vocals in Netflix’s animated Castlevania: Nocturne). A Grammy award winner and sought-after New York vocalist, he has performed on some of the world’s finest concert stages, including Radio City Music Hall, Avery Fisher Hall (with the New York Philharmonic), and Carnegie Hall. He’s toured North America from coast to coast and played sold-out stadiums from Australia to Indonesia to Puerto Rico. In 2017 he was the featured artist at the US Open Finals live broadcast honoring the 50th anniversary of Billie Jean King’s Triple Crown. He opened the Museum of the American Revolution (introducing then-Vice President Joe Biden), was invited to perform at the White House at their Children’s Educational initiative, and is a go-to for tailoring unique theatrical/concert performances at corporate events and charitable galas.
