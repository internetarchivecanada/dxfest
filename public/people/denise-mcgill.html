    Biography

    Denise McGill (Producer/Director, Gullah Gone) is a photojournalist with a focus on migration, faith and multicultural issues. She worked as a staff photographer for daily newspapers until earning a spot as an overseas correspondent for the award-winning magazine The Commission. She went on assignment to 25 countries in three years. She now shoots photos and video, and teaches at University of South Carolina’s School of Journalism & Mass Communications.
