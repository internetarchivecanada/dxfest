Biography

Nima Fatemi is an independent security researcher, focused on encryption, privacy and censorship circumvention technologies. Fatemi is a passionate advocate who focuses on the education and empowerment of everyday people to protect their privacy online.

When he is not tracking down state-sponsored hackers or finding ways to bypass censorship, he spends time with activists and grassroots organizations to help them secure their information from various adversaries. With almost a decade of experience providing rapid response and security training to people at risk globally, Fatemi is well positioned to understand a community’s specific needs, and to help make encryption technology usable and accessible for the general public.
