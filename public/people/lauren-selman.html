Biography

Lauren is currently the Director of Conference Services, Industry Relations and Communications for Hostec Events with experience in communications and social media marketing. She has executed event management and public relations campaigns for a wide variety of clients, counting among her recent work successful efforts with the AFI Docs Film Festival.

Previously, Lauren coordinated materials for the cast and crews of national touring theatre productions at Troika Entertainment. She has conducted press outreach for AFI Docs (formerly AFI-Discovery Channel Silverdocs Documentary Festival), The President’s Committee on the Art’s and Humanities, College of Health & Human Development (HHD) at Penn State University, as well as several book and documentary releases. She has executed PR and marketing outreach for various clients, which garnered earned media hits in The New York Times, The Washington Post, ABC, NBC, CBS and National Public Radio.
