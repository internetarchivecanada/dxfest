Pete Earley is the author of, most recently, Crazy: A Father’s Search through America’s Mental Health Madness and The Hot House: Life Inside Leavenworth Prison. His book Family of Spies: Inside the John Walker Spy Ring was a New York Times bestseller and was made into a CBS miniseries.
