Biography

[vc_row][vc_column][vc_column_text]Mariana Brito returns to Double Exposure as festival manager after assisting at the first edition of the event as festival coordinator and graphic designer.

She has worked at Nova Studios integrating a graphic design team and at 100Reporters as Social Media trainee. Brito also managed the Marketing Department at Rumos, Brazil, where she developed the visual identity and planned marketing strategies for the company.

Mariana is a graduate of the Universidade Federal de Juiz de Fora, Brazil, and has a second degree in graphic design at Northern Virginia Community College.
