Biography

Matthew Iles is an expert in positioning brands for breakout success. His agency Nine Lines led marketing for more than forty consumer startups over six years, and he co-created the digital marketing course for General Assembly. He then founded the blockchain-for-journalism startup Civil, which gained more than two hundred press mentions across global media outlets and was acquired by ConsenSys in 2020. Matthew co-founded Serotonin to partner with breakthrough technology companies on bringing remarkable new ideas and products to the world.
