Biography

Multidisciplinary artist, truth-teller, and designer, Ashley M. Freeby, uses natural/neutral materials, poetic language and minimalism to explore family, site, and challenge our memories of black and brown bodies that have been taking unjustly. Born in rural Pennsylvania (b. 1986) Freeby currently lives/work in Chicago, IL. Her recent solo and dual exhibitions include ‘Plots & Hems‘ at Hyde Park Art Center (2019), ‘As gesture‘ at Chicago Artist Coalition (2019), ‘Non-constants‘ at The Annex @ Spudnik Press (2019), and ‘Unjustified Patterns‘ at Kanzlei, Berlin (2018). In addition, she has participated in numerous group exhibitions in Chicago, Toronto Canada, and Pennsylvania. She is currently a Halcyon Arts Lab Fellow in DC, a HATCH Projects Resident in Chicago, and was previously in residency at the Vermont Studio Center and the Institut für Alles Mögliche in Berlin Germany. Freeby was recently awarded the SPARK Microgrant and has earned a MFA from the School of the Art Institute of Chicago (2018) and a BA from Bucknell University (2015).

Other bios (1)

Ashley M Freeby is a multidisciplinary artist who was born in rural Pennsylvania and currently lives in Chicago, IL. Freeby uses her voice to project truth telling works through a multitude of mediums. Focusing on the history of land, she questions who worked it, who owned it and the accessibility of it. Freeby uses natural materials, poetic language and minimalist aesthetics to explore sites of trauma and challenge our perceptions of memory of lives lost. She earned a MFA from the School of the Art Institute of Chicago (2018) and a BA from Bucknell University (2015).
