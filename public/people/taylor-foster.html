Biography

[vc_row][vc_column][vc_column_text]

After working as a volunteer for the inaugural Double Exposure festival in 2015, Taylor has received a degree in Cinematography at Tribeca Flashpoint College in Chicago, IL.

He has since worked around the country pursuing various creative outlets. In 2016, Taylor worked with Sender Films, a production company internationally recognized for their outdoor adventure films, in the editing department. Currently, you can find Taylor working as a freelance photographer, specializing in editorial photography.

With ample experience in organizing production teams, he will transfer those skills to his position as Volunteer Coordinator, enlisting volunteers for the 2017 festival.
