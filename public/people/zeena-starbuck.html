Biography

Zeena Starbuck is the Project Coordinator for Doc Society’s Democracy Story Unit (DSU) and Lead Researcher & Coordinator for the DISCO Network’s Independence Project. With the DSU, she works on the development and implementation of the Democracy Story Labs and narrative strategy projects, including the Democracy Story Lab London and Big Tech Narrative Initiative. With the Independence Project, she works closely with filmmakers and documentary organisations from around the world to conduct interviews, publish research outputs, and facilitate events to further the discussion around the global documentary ecosystem.
