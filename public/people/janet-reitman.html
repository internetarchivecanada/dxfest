Biography

Janet Reitman is an investigative journalist and contributing writer for The New York Times Magazine, as well as a contributing editor at Rolling Stone, covering, extremism, terrorism, and national security. Since starting her career in the mid-1990s, Reitman has covered conflicts in Africa, as well as the war in Iraq, reported from Moscow on Vladimir Putin’s crackdown on the press, and has traveled across the U.S. reporting stories on a wide range of subjects, from the politics of the Tea Party and the Christian Right, to the exploits of Edward Snowden and Chelsea Manning, the 2013 Boston Marathon bombing, ISIS, and most recently, the young men drawn into the politics of the far right. Reitman has twice been a reporting finalist for the National Magazine Award, most recently for her 2013 profile of Boston Marathon bomber Dzhokhar Tsarnaev. She is also author of The New York Times Best Seller and The New York Times Notable Book, Inside Scientology (2011). Reitman currently working on a book about the far right for Spiegel & Grau/Random House.
