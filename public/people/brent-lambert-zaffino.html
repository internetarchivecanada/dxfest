Biography

Brent Lambert-Zaffino is a filmmaker and film festival veteran. He created and directs the Peaberry Film Festival in Canton, Georgia, formerly known as the Etowah Film Festival, located at the Southern border of the Appalachia. He is the director of the 2021 Southern Gothic feature Lost Cause, and of the short film Cooking Inside: From Prison to Home.
