Biography

Susanne Reber is a Peabody award-winning investigative editor and executive producer. She is currently the executive producer of podcasts at Scripps Washington Bureau, where she created Verified, a new investigative podcast documentary series, launched in February 2020. She is the co-creator and former executive editor of Reveal, the first investigative radio show and podcast in the U.S. Prior to Reveal, Susanne founded and led NPR’s first investigative unit.

Susanne will be facilitating the Sound Waves: Tools and Tips for Turning Print Investigations Into Riveting Podcasts workshop and will be featured in Crossing Borders.

Other bios (2)

Susanne Reber is an executive producer and investigative editor with more than thirty years of storytelling experience in audio and multimedia. She is the co-founder and former executive editor of the Peabody Award-winning investigative radio show Reveal and creator and executive producer of Verified, a serialized investigative podcast now in its third season. Susanne is the founder of NPR’s investigations team and founded and led CBC Radio’s Michener Award-winning investigative team prior to moving to the United States. She splits her time between Washington, DC and Switzerland.

Susanne Reber is an award-winning investigative editor, executive producer and author. She is currently leading the podcast team at the E. W. Scripps Washington Bureau. Her work has garnered her three Peabody Awards, an Emmy award, and a DuPont award, among many others, and has been showcased in national and international film festivals. She is the co-founder of Reveal and the former executive editor of the Peabody Award-winning investigative radio program and podcast. Prior to joining Scripps she held leadership roles at The Center for Investigative Reporting.
