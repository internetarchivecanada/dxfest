Biography

Tamara Gould is the Senior Vice President of National Production and Strategic Partnerships at Independent Television Service (ITVS) where she oversees numerous documentaries and series in all stages of development. Tamara directs multiple strategic initiatives designed to connect powerful storytelling from around the world with new distribution platforms and innovative engagement approaches. Tamara is an award winning filmmaker and former Executive Director of BAVC and Executive Producer at KQED in San Francisco. She graduated from Brown University with an Honors Bachelors Degree in Anthropology and Film and received her Masters in Cinema and Social Change from UC Santa Cruz.
