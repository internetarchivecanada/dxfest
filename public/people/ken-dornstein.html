Biography

Ken Dornstein is the director of MY BROTHER’S BOMBER, a three-part documentary about his attempt to find the men responsible for the 1988 bombing of Pan Am Flight 103, which aired on PBS FRONTLINE last year.  He also produced HAPPY VALLEY, about the Jerry Sandusky scandal at Penn State, which premiered at Sundance in 2014.  He worked for more than a decade at PBS FRONTLINE as a senior produced.
