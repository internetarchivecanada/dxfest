Biography

Arden Fitzpatrick is the Manager of DX Access this year at Double Exposure Film Festival and Symposium. This past May, she graduated from Georgetown University with a major in Spanish and a minor in Film & Media Studies. Last year, Arden had the opportunity to serve as Double Exposure’s Volunteer Manager, where she planned recruitment and outreach efforts to attract volunteers in the greater D.C. area.

While in college, she was a staff writer for the student newspaper, The Hoya’s, Guide section, where she loved writing film and cultural event reviews, that were then published in print and online. She also deeply enjoyed minoring in Film & Media Studies, where she was able to let her creativity run wild and had the freedom to develop and execute many of her film concepts. For her Senior year capstone project, Arden, along with two other fellow minors, wrote and produced a thriller podcast, titled Watc(her), focused on a woman’s perception of the truth and how it begins to unravel.

She is so excited to be taking on a larger role this year and can’t wait for Double Exposure 2020!
