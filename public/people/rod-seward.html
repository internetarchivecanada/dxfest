    Biography

    Rod Seward is a former Royal Marines Commando and for the last twenty years has been a safety/security advisor to international news media. He mainly provides on-the-ground security advice but is also as an instructor on various hostile environment training courses. He has worked in Afghanistan, Iraq, and Israel, and he recently covered Hurricane Florence.
