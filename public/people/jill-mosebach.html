    Biography

    Jill Mosebach is a nonprofit fundraising executive with 10+ years of experience in resource mobilization, relationship management, grant stewardship, and mission-driven communications. As Senior Director of Institutional Partnerships at Tribeca Film Institute (TFI), she cultivates and liaises with key institutional funders, generates $1.8 million in annual revenue for TFI’s Artist, Interactive, and Education Programs, and helps to oversee the Institute’s strategic planning efforts. Prior to joining TFI in 2015, Jill served as Associate Director of Development at Historic Hudson Valley, a museum consortium founded by John D. Rockefeller, Jr., where she helped to expand the organization’s digital and education programs focusing on the history of U.S. colonial slavery in the North. Previously, she served as Senior Grant Writer for Helen Keller International, a global NGO that works to prevent blindness and malnutrition worldwide. Jill earned her Bachelor of Journalism from the University of Missouri-Columbia and completed graduate coursework in international human rights at New York University. She is a New York Community Trust Leadership Fellow and Guest Lecturer at Baruch College’s Austin W. Marxe School of Public and International Affairs.
