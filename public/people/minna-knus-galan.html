Biography

Minna Knus-Galán is a reporter on the investigative team MOT at the Finnish Broadcasting Company, Yle. Her coverage of money laundering, Bangladesh garment factories and corruption has earned several awards, including 2015 Journalist of the Year, among others.

Knus-Galán has participated in multiple cross-border projects and is the writer of the book Projekt Panama (Swedish and Finnish) about the Pulitzer Prize-winning Panama Papers and how the project brought down barriers between journalists and documentary filmmakers. She is a member of ICIJ.
