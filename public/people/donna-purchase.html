Biography

Donna Purchase is an event and volunteer manager professional with experience managing volunteer programs for corporate and non-profit companies. Her most recent experience was Volunteer Manager for The AFI DOCS Film Festival where she recruited, trained and managed more than 250 volunteers.

She previously managed the volunteer program for The Fannie Mae Foundation serving more than 5,000 employees across the United States.

Donna is a true advocate of volunteerism. A resident of the District of Columbia, she serves on the board of 5 non-profits and is known in the community as a go-to person when there is a need for volunteers.
