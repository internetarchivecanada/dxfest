    Biography

    Anabelle Rojas is the production manager in charge for all programming strands of Al Jazeera English in Washington, DC. She spent the earlier part of her career of more than 10 years in a variety of roles within the Finance department of the Al Jazeera Media Network. She provided financial support to programs management by working closely with the production teams of long- form documentaries, children’s programming and digital productions to ensure all projects stayed within budget. She also collaborated with the Director of Business Affairs by ensuring transparent and efficient contracting of independent production companies, and freelance contractors working on programs projects. In her current position, she has oversight of all operational and financial matters for in-house and commission productions as well as discussion programs. Rojas partners with the editorial management to ensure the smooth operations from pre-production, budget developing and monitoring and cost reporting to successful delivery of all Al Jazeera English productions out of Washington, DC.
