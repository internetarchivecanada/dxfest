Biography

Rosana is a communications pro and event planner with more than 20 years of dreaming up and executing events. Her experience includes logistics for fashion, political, and corporate events. She is a skilled strategist, designing events through easy-to-follow timelines, task lists, deadlines, streamlined decision-making, and team collaboration that, together, bring a client’s vision to life. She also provides onsite event coordination to ensure a smooth finish. She has worked on events with a broad range of organizations including Urban Alliance, Asian Pacific American Institute for Congressional Studies (APAICS), Economic Security Project (ESP), Bloomingdale’s, and more. Rosana was born in Austin, Texas, the daughter of Thai immigrants who moved to the U.S. for school…and stayed. She has a master’s degree in journalism and worked as a writer and editor. Her entry into event planning started entrepreneurially. She was the founder of an award-winning fashion business, working with thousands of clients one-on-one and inside a members-only fashion community.

She has lived overseas in Bangkok, Thailand and out West in Colorado. Today, she is based in her childhood hometown – Silver Spring, Maryland – where she lives with her husband, three children, and dog, Rocky.
