    Biography

    Wendy Benjaminson has been a journalist, reporting and editing in Washington, DC, and Houston for more than 30 years. She is the managing editor for McClatchy’s Washington bureau. She joined McClatchy earlier this year after 12 years at The Associated Press, where she served as Texas News editor, national security editor and assistant bureau chief, and acting bureau chief. She was also a managing editor for government and politics at USA TODAY, and over 13 years, she worked in a number of positions at the Houston Chronicle, including politics editor and Metro editor to assistant managing editor for Sunday and special projects.

    A 1983 graduate of the University of Maryland, Benjaminson has also covered Virginia state politics and government, Congress, the White House, trade issues and overseen coverage of the Pentagon, State Department and US policy in the Middle East, including Afghanistan, Iraq and Syria. Benjaminson has been nominated five times in six years for the Pulitzer Prize.
