Biography

John is a veteran communications professional with executive experience across issues and industries with missions to build progressive social and behavioral change movements. He is the founder of the award-winning arts and culture non-profit, BloomBars. Prior to founding BloomBars, John was a Senior Vice President at the global communication and advocacy firm, GMMB, where his client roster included the Bill & Melinda Gates Foundation, The Fetzer Institute, Casey Family Programs, and the national Click It Or Ticket seat belt mobilization.

Prior to joining GMMB, John was the Director of Communication for the Presidential Members of the U.S. Census Monitoring Board — charged with monitoring and promoting the largest peacetime mobilization in U.S. History. In previous roles, John served as the General Manager of the Capitol Radio Network where he built a network of more than 100 urban radio stations nationwide. He was also a Senior Account Manager at Walls Communications where his clients included the NAACP, National Urban League, and The Leadership Conference on Civil Rights. John considers himself a multi-hyphenate creative with a passion for visionary storytelling and communications strategy. He is a published essayist, and has worked in the documentary film and entertainment space for more than a decade.
