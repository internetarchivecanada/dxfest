Biography

Christine Richard is president of Orion Research LLC, which does research for investors and has investigated Herbalife Ltd. for more than three years. Prior to founding Orion Research, she worked as a reporter covering financial markets from New York, Washington, Hong Kong and Singapore for news organizations including Bloomberg News, Dow Jones and Institutional Investor. She is the author of Confidence Game: How Hedge Fund Manager Bill Ackman Called Wall Street’s Bluff, a book about the collapse of the bond insurance business ahead of the financial crisis.
