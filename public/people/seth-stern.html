Biography

Seth Stern is the chief of advocacy at Freedom of the Press Foundation (FPF). Seth and FPF’s advocacy team work in collaboration with everyone from independent bloggers and incarcerated journalists to major national news outlets and civil liberties organizations to promote laws and policies that allow public-interest journalism to thrive. Seth’s articles about press freedom have been published by outlets like The Guardian, The Intercept, Rolling Stone and Columbia Journalism Review, as well as local newspapers in his hometown of Chicago and around the country. Before joining FPF, Seth practiced media and First Amendment law for 13 years.

Other bios (1)

Seth Stern directs the Freedom of the Press Foundation, overseeing support for journalists and whistleblowers under fire. He practiced media and First Amendment law for 13 years at a Chicago firm. Seth chairs the Chicago Bar Association's Media and Entertainment Law Committee and the American Bar Association's Media, Privacy and Advertising Law Committee.
