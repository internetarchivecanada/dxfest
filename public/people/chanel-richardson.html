Biography

Chanel Richardson is currently a Technical Program Manager within Google Cloud’s Information Security Department (Cloud CISO). She works on data strategies and solutions for the organization.

Outside of work, Chanel explores technologies and has a strong interest in the ephemera of the internet. She is exploring many ways to remix public domain works that can be found on the Internet Archive. One such set of explorations has led to the creation of “Archive Radio”, a series of composite videos, available on YouTube, combining footage and music found on the archive.
