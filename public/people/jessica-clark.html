Other bios (1)

Jessica Clark is founder and director of media strategy and production firm Dot Connector Studio. She serves as research director of Media Impact Funders, a network of foundations that support public interest media. Clark is editor of Immerse.news, an online publication produced by TFI, MIT’s Open Doc Lab and the Fledgling Fund to spark discussion about emerging nonfiction storytelling.
